# Protected two-wire pack screen, 2026-10-05

The owner accepted a **trial board-mounted battery-facing NTC** under [ADR 0019](../decisions/0019-board-mounted-battery-temperature-sensing.md), with cell-to-board thermal validation before battery/board freeze. This permits two-wire packs without bypassing hardware temperature qualification. ADR 0024 now selects the exact `LP452845`/PHR-2 drawing as the Rev A design target; no battery is ordered or qualified. Dimensions and ratings below are manufacturer/distributor claims; runtimes are calculations, not measurements.

The provisional finished-pack envelope is ≤32.0 mm wide and ≤6.1 mm thick. The reference calculation uses 0.45 W, 3.7 V nominal and 85% usable energy. The existing modeled full-white case is 2.49 W, or 0.83 A at 3.0 V, before transient margin. All final dimensions require CAD fit, including the protection board, wire exit, connector and swelling space.

Next sourcing decision: ask LiPol for current stock, a quote, connector polarity and a continuous discharge rating for the exact `LP452845`/PHR-2 assembly. In parallel, request an exact keyed-connector assembly code and revised drawing for `LP503055`, or find a distributor-stocked equivalent with documented finished dimensions and continuous discharge margin above the modeled 0.83 A full-white draw. A connector option is a distinct assembly, not a silent substitution for the drawn bare-wire part. The PCB NTC must pass ADR 0019's cell-to-board comparison. No charger-current ceiling is raised by this screen.

**2026-10-06 availability recheck:** DigiKey still lists the exact GlobTek coupon-only part as [in stock](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/globtek-inc/BL0750F5030481S1PCTC/16515787). Two other orderable possibilities do not replace the final-pack inquiry: Jauch's 950 mAh [LP523450JU family](https://www.jauch.com/en-US/products/battery_technology/getPrm/batteries/Lithium%20Polymer%20Batteries/) is 34.8 mm wide before a connector, beyond the provisional 32.0 mm battery-width ceiling; TinyCircuits [ASR00036](https://files.tinycircuits.com/Datasheets/ASR00036_850mAh.pdf) is 850 mAh but allows only 0.425 A continuous charge/discharge and its [finished thickness range](https://tinycircuits.com/products/copy-of-lithium-ion-polymer-battery-3-7v-850mah) reaches 6.5 mm. The GlobTek pack is therefore still the only immediately orderable coupon candidate in this screen, while LP503055 remains the leading final-badge inquiry. Stock is time-sensitive; this is a sourcing observation, not a pack selection or purchase authorization.

**2026-10-09 drawing correction:** LiPol's exact [LP452845 JST PHR-2 assembly drawing](https://www.lipobattery.us/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/LP452845-3.7V-900mAh-with-JST-PHR-2.pdf) is available. It makes this the best *documented connectorized fit candidate* so far, while `LP503055` retains a higher stated continuous-discharge rating and slightly higher modeled runtime. Neither has confirmed current stock or a fully qualified final-badge discharge/thermal/connector fit. Do not interpret the seller's conditional one-week sample lead time as confirmed availability.

**2026-10-09 design decision:** [ADR 0024](../decisions/0024-lp452845-pack-and-temperature-window.md) chooses the exact LP452845/PHR-2 assembly for source capture and adds a battery-facing hardware 15/40 °C charge-permission guard. This resolves which pack to design around; it does not assert a continuous 900 mA rating, stock, physical fit or a validated cell-to-board temperature bound. The [revised swap screen](../../mechanical/lp452845-swap-screen-2026-10-09.md) uses the actual JST footprint courtyard rather than the too-small provisional PH pocket.
