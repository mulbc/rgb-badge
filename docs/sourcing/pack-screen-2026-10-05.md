# Protected two-wire pack screen, 2026-10-05

The owner accepted a **trial board-mounted battery-facing NTC** under [ADR 0019](../decisions/0019-board-mounted-battery-temperature-sensing.md), with cell-to-board thermal validation before battery/board freeze. This permits two-wire packs without bypassing hardware temperature qualification. No battery is selected or ordered. Dimensions and ratings below are manufacturer/distributor claims; runtimes are calculations, not measurements.

The provisional finished-pack envelope is ≤32.0 mm wide and ≤6.1 mm thick. The reference calculation uses 0.45 W, 3.7 V nominal and 85% usable energy. The existing modeled full-white case is 2.49 W, or 0.83 A at 3.0 V, before transient margin. All final dimensions require CAD fit, including the protection board, wire exit, connector and swelling space.
