All values below are manufacturer/distributor claims or calculations, not measurements.

## Screen against current project constraints

For the **final** 48×16 badge the trial mechanical section allows an entire terminated pack at most **6.1 mm** thick, and a trial pack-width ceiling of **32.0 mm** after case walls and side clearances. These are provisional and omit detailed PCM/lead exit, attachment and assembly fit. The provisional 0.45 W reference load at 3.7 V and 85% usable nominal energy gives a **~859 mAh arithmetic floor** for six hours; the modeled 2.49 W full-white load needs about **0.83 A at 3.0 V**, before transient margin. The former 80% in 45–60 minutes / full in 75–100 minutes targets were withdrawn by ADR 0016; pack-specific times require documentation and measurement.

## Evidence needed to select

Request **one revision-controlled PDF per exact terminated pack/order code**, including: maximum length/width/thickness over shrink-wrap and PCM plus lead-exit/connector local peaks and swelling/installation allowance; actual mass; 1S chemistry/maximum charge voltage; minimum and nominal delivered capacity at defined discharge/cutoff conditions; maximum charge current including temperature derating and continuous/peak discharge ratings; protection thresholds and OFF quiescent/leakage specification; 10k NTC B value, tolerance, physical location, ground-reference wiring and TS trip limits; connector housing/terminals and *front/back pin numbering/polarity*; safety/transport test evidence for that assembly, sample MOQ, US-shipped lead time and traceable supply. Specifically ask GlobTek to reconcile the `BL0700`/`BL0750` drawing labels and state the NTC curve, and ask LiPol whether an `LP452845` 3-wire NTC+PCM version is available with **max assembled thickness ≤6.1 mm**, **max width ≤32 mm** and an exact order code. Ask the supplier to state clearly whether 900 mA is continuous discharge and whether a higher safe charge rate exists for that *specific cell*; do not infer it from the bare-cell code.

Do not raise ISET, omit the pack NTC, squeeze the pouch to fit, or buy a hypothetical custom configuration. A coupon battery and final battery may be different **only with separate charger/TS configurations and documented board population**. No KiCad source or requirements changed in this sourcing screen. Next electrical step after receiving a qualified pack drawing: set the exact ISET/NTC/timer parts, verify worst-case input/thermal and charge-time corners, then capture pack connector and physical switch in KiCad for native review and Gate A.
