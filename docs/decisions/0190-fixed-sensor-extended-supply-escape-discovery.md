# ADR 0190: Fixed sensor extended supply escape discovery

Date: 2026-11-16
Status: Accepted for read-only complete-interface discovery only.

ADR0189 finds 33 clear selector captures along original-route prefixes, but
none of its original short U39 supply escapes clears mutually. Retain all
parts, poses, capacitor associations and the exact 80-item prospective private
selector cohort. Read-only broaden the fixed U39.5 ordinary 0.20 mm B escape:
horizontal from (56.885,15.500) to x=56.050–56.550 mm, then vertical to
y=13.500–18.000 mm, sampled every 0.050 mm, total length at most 3.500 mm.

access with actual foreign/no-net copper, all other source items, actual
board edge and full-board hole policy unchanged. Record layer-specific
obstructions and mutually clear cohorts against the 33 exact selector
prefix/capture choices. No narrower trace or new unsupported transition.

Write no PCB. Clear escapes alone do not establish either selector or supply
continuity. Separate exact complete-prototype scope and all original groups,
interfaces, matching reduced counts, exact104, source/replay/inverse/contact/
hole/orphan/fault/visual gates precede selection. No electrical, threshold,
current, protection, thermal, C51/U40 locality, stack/process/fit/Gate A or
fabrication hold changes.
