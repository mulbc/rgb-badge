# ADR 0166: Native diagonal and grid-phase continuation

Date: 2026-11-15
Status: Accepted for ignored search refinement; no successor selected.

Continue only the exact ADR 0164 heartbeat access prototype, preserving the
immutable 29-open parent's groups/items and all prior routing restrictions.
Cardinal-only F search exhausts its sampled component; the layered searches
reach their bounds. Those graph results do not establish geometric infeasibility.

Permit eight-neighbour planar exploration with native 0.20 mm track-shape
checks on every diagonal segment, even when both endpoint masks are clear.
Charge diagonal steps sqrt(2) times the axis step cost. Scale the existing
four-neighbour empty-grid heuristic by 1/sqrt(2), a conservative eight-neighbour
lower bound, before the existing weighted search factor; claim no optimality.
Do not change the immutable public predecessor algorithms or their fingerprints.

Permit 0.050 mm grids with original or 0.025 mm phase offsets, and a 0.025 mm
fine grid. Recompute dimensions, masks and actual endpoint connectors from
each exact grid; keep all native source/mutual copper, hole, 0.500 mm edge,
width/via/type and filled-group checks. Record origin/step, neighbours and
heuristic semantics. Keep the 1,200,000-node/120 mm total signal bounds per
attempt. All F-only/F-In1 scopes and ADR 0165's transit-only In1, 24 mm total /
4 mm segment In2 bounds remain unchanged where applicable. No deeper layer,
new rule, source retirement/move or centre microvia is authorized.

A complete path still needs matching native/physical reduction below 29, every
one of the original 217 groups/items, full four-terminal heartbeat continuity,
exact original 104 findings and all source/contact/hole/replay/inverse/orphan/
fault/visual gates. No current/protection/NTC/timer/default-off or fabrication
boundary changes; every electrical/part/process/stack/fit/Gate A hold remains.
