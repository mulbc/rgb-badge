# ADR 0203: Contact-preserving resistor translation discovery

Date: 2026-11-16
Status: Accepted for read-only native geometry discovery only.

Fixed-centre rotations alone yield no complete feed. From the immutable
selected 28-open source, independently sample exact R71 ERJ2RKF8873X and
R21/R11 ERJ2RKF1001X at cardinal orientations; additionally R21 at 15 and
165 degrees. Sample translations of each original connected pad-2 centre
within ±0.250 mm on each axis at 0.025 mm intervals. Every old direct
source-layer copper contact and every old filled pad-2 contact must physically
touch the moved pad; fixed centre equality is replaced only by this stronger
complete-interface check. Keep all original copper and every other part fixed.

Require exact local-pad/field/part/net identity, native foreign/no-net copper,
all fixed courtyards and actual original copper-edge rules. Screen direct
ordinary 0.20 mm B necks ≤0.750 mm to supported 0.30/0.10 B/In4 and aligned
In3/In4 captures sampled at 0.050 mm offsets around supply pad 1. Require
actual contact with original main-group same-net copper on a capture layer
or an individually witnessed original main same-net In3 filled unit and every source/mutual copper and full-board hole check. A clear
landing in a disconnected same-net trace or plane island is not useful evidence.
The source has no connected In3 plane unit for these three supplies; actual
retained main-group copper is therefore the applicable target.

Write no PCB. No source retirement, current/value change, smaller copper,
unsupported centre transition or rule exception. Every useful exact pose
requires separate complete-prototype scope, original-group/native/physical/
exact104/source/replay/inverse/contact/hole/fault/visual gates and all existing
electrical/part/thermal/bypass/stack/process/fit/Gate A/fabrication holds.
