# ADR 0088: Exact dual-converter routing selection at 50 opens

Date: 2026-11-05
Status: Accepted for the selected 50-edge CAD routing checkpoint; hardware qualification remains open.

ADRs 0086/0087 find a clear U36/L1 pair and restore its two old supply groups.
The bounded cleanup retires eleven exact old items (four original-215 and seven
accepted additions), including only the documented shared-leaf tail replacement.
The final ignored trial has 50 native/physical opens and exact 104 intrinsic
findings, SHA-256 6bcd317299c6a0771799d12f054087e009a6ad5ba9ae5423362e4d2bfb47c2c6.

Extend ADR 0085's exact child ledger with only these two new poses, four checked
short switching tracks, the successful bounded ordinary-width supply additions,
eleven exact retirements and retained SYS tail. Derive the final multiset
directly against the immutable corrected 58-edge parent. Freeze the earlier
52-edge VLED plan unchanged and retain explicit replay support for both stages.
The cumulative ledger must contain 15 poses, 65 retirements (20 original and
45 accepted) and 134 additions. Keep all exact parts, canonical pads/fields,
other poses, zones/structure/rules/profiles, current/protection/enable settings
and firmware unchanged. No further retirement or width exception is authorized.

Before selecting the 50-edge child, require exact independent inventory/origin/
pose/pad/net checks, all 246 parent UUID pad groups retained, original/new VLED
pin joins, both U36/L1 numbered switching joins and its old SYS/output group
restoration. Enforce calculated short-node path lengths separately from opens.
Require 50 native/physical opens, exact unchanged 104 intrinsic findings, three
stable fills, identical replays, meaningful faults, source contract and original
three-orphan restoration producing only the same three dangling warnings.
Preserve every active shared-leaf interface and its bottom bus. Extend normal
native/source-only acceptance entry points without relaxing the parent gate.

The local switching-node feasibility/routing-study goal is met only when that
gate passes. This does not close the remaining 50 opens or qualify power-loop
width/current, bypass/feedback placement, uncompleted ground/control connections,
electrical/thermal/timing, stencil/HDI/assembly/RF/enclosure or exact procurement.
The earlier 50-edge planning target is not a fabrication criterion. Hardware is
unbuilt; independent Gate A remains required and fabrication prohibited.
