# ADR 0026: Isolate charger-gate inhibition from USB logic-ready

Date: 2026-10-11. Status: selected for provisional Rev A capture; operating qualification remains open.

Use the exact `TPS259474ARPWR` as E1, between the future E0 protected USB rail and BQ24074 IN. Its pin 1 `EN/UVLO` is the common `CHARGER_GATE_EN` node. Drive it only from the USB 3.3 V logic domain through an exact Panasonic `ERJ2RKF2203X` 220 kΩ pull-up and `ERJ2RKF2703X` 270 kΩ pull-down. The passive divider makes EN low with that rail absent; no raw VBUS or battery voltage drives EN. Use three independent `SN74AUP1G125DBVR` active-low OE sinks with A grounded and Y tied to EN: the already captured U40 for `TEMP_OK`, a new source-permission sink controlled by `USB_CHARGE_REQ`, and a new supervisor sink controlled by U34's `USB_RAW_LOGIC_READY`. Each new AUP has its own exact `GRM155R71C104KA88D` 100 nF bypass. A sink is active when its controlling signal is low, so E1 can turn on only after all three release. E1's output joins the existing BQ `CHARGER_IN_DRAFT` source net; its input remains a clearly marked protected-USB draft boundary until E0 is captured. Do not use U23's `USB_EN1_RAW_N` test output as an actuator.

Keep the supervisor RESET signal distinct from E1 EN. Revalue U34 RESET's R78 pull-up from 10 kΩ to exact `ERJ2RKF1003X` 100 kΩ and its R63 `USB_RAW_LOGIC_READY` pull-down from 100 kΩ to exact `ERJ2RKF1004X` 1 MΩ. This reduces nominal pull-up current at 0.8 V from 80 µA to 8 µA before other loads, below TI's 15 µA power-up RESET test load. At a nominal 3.3 V rail their unloaded divider leaves 3.0 V for U27's Schmitt input and the new AUP OE. Recheck both paths with leakage, rail and resistor corners in the connected circuit; the 0.8 V RESET condition also requires TI's specified supply-rise rate.

Set E1 ILM with exact `ERJ2RKF3321X` 3.32 kΩ to ground, with ITIMER open for the fastest breaker response. TI's published 3.32 kΩ test point has a 0.850–1.150 A breaker threshold; that is a device test condition, not a guaranteed complete source limit after resistor drift. BQ's existing lower fixed ILIM remains the charger input-current ceiling. Leave E1 OVLO grounded because the upstream E0 protector is to perform voltage admission; leave unused PGTH/PG explicitly unconnected. Add local E1 input bypass `GRM155R71C104KA88D` 100 nF/16 V and a close output reservoir `GRM187R61A226ME15` 22 µF/10 V, in addition to the existing BQ IN capacitor. Use exact `GRM1555C1H222GA01D` 2.2 nF C0G from E1 dVdt to ground. TI's idealized equation gives about 0.91 V/ms slew with 2.2 nF; actual inrush, effective capacitor values and turn-on timing remain to be verified.

The reproducible [DC screen](../../tools/check-charger-gate-screen.py) includes ±1% initial resistance and ±100 ppm/K over a 65 K displacement. Reserving 1.6 µA total EN-node current for three released AUP outputs and the E1 pin gives **at least 1.434 V** at a 3.0 V rail in the adverse sinking direction, above E1's 1.223 V maximum rising threshold. At a 1.65 V rail and adverse sourcing direction it gives **at most 1.116 V**, below E1's 1.183 V minimum rising threshold. These margins are **calculations under an assumed aggregate current**, not a manufacturer-guaranteed startup proof; individual leakages are specified at different conditions. Partial-rail behavior, PCB leakage, U34/U27 loading, fault transients and the actual E0 output must be checked before describing the gate as fail-safe. This decision does not raise any USB or charging current ceiling and does not approve fabrication.
