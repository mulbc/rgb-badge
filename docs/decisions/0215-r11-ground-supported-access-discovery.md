# ADR 0215: R11 ground supported access discovery

Date: 2026-11-19
Status: Accepted for read-only native discovery only.

ADR0214 straight/elbow and eight-neighbour B searches restore neither
PAIR07.1 nor PAIR08.1. Their bounded B graphs exhaust at 1,155 and 1,573
nodes respectively. The exact middle-pose prototype remains rejected.

At the same ordinary B nodes reachable within 3 mm lead length and 2.5 mm
radius, discover supported 0.30/0.10 mm B/In4 captures and optional aligned
In3/In4 captures. Check all foreign/no-net source copper, actual moved
R11 pads and both new leads, mutual captures and full-board source hole
policy. No new centre/through via, smaller feature, part move or additional
source retirement is allowed. Prefer actual retained main-GND copper contact;
a clear gateway without that contact remains incomplete and unselected.

Write no PCB. Any eventual complete ordinary ground restoration requires a
separate exact frozen plan and every ADR0212 original-group/contact/source/
serialized/replay/inverse/hole/orphan/fault/visual gate, matching 26 native/
physical opens and exact104. No electrical/return/thermal/bypass/stack/process/
fit/Gate A or fabrication qualification is inferred or relaxed; all current
and protection/default-off boundaries remain fixed.
