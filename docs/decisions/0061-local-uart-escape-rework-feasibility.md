# ADR 0061: Local UART escape rework feasibility

Date: 2026-10-28
Status: Exact seven-track source chain selected at 62 edges after the complete acceptance gate.

The accepted source remains 63 opens. A UART0_TX route through unchanged 0.12 mm digital-width geometry can join R47.2 to TP4, but its resistor-side B escape isolates the previously connected R82 ground group. A short 0.20 mm application-supply bridge restores the other disrupted group. Fine local ground bridges and a six-layer 0.20 mm ground search do not restore R82. Moving only the new UART escape/vias has not passed the group gate.

A direct R47.2 B-to-In4 adjacent microvia would avoid the long rear escape, but original UART0_TX_RAW In4 tracks obstruct that site. The original ROW_04_A In3 copper also blocks a full via stack; keep it fixed by using only the adjacent B/In4 transition at the resistor. Permit an ignored exact local rework of only the seven original 0.20 mm UART0_TX_RAW In4 staircase tracks near x = 51.35–52.35 mm, y = 8.85–9.00 mm. Enumerate original signatures and every retained external same-layer contact before removing anything. Preserve every other original 215-edge copper item, every source via and all accepted additions, every part/pose/pin/value and every prior physical group. The candidate must retain exact 0.20 mm RAW width/clearance; only the separately reviewed UART0_TX digital side may use 0.12 mm under ADR 0047's 120 mm bound. Existing USB status inventory and supply necks stay unchanged.

Do not substitute or move the exact 499 Ω R47 or its lands. The route change crosses no resistor or protection/enable boundary and changes no output drive, current limit, mode or firmware. Bound the local RAW replacement to its retired length plus 3 mm, retain each external contact on In4, and report calculated new lengths. No power/ground/row/colour/USB differential or CC copper may be retired. Failed geometry, split plane groups or new native findings reject the transaction.

Canonical selection would require a new exact seven-track original-chain replacement policy, frozen 63-edge reference, independent non-retirement proof for every other source item and direct external-contact proof, negative tests against unlisted deletion or lost contacts, unchanged complete 104 findings, lower independent/native opens, three stable refills, deterministic replay, source/LED evidence and unchanged orphan restoration. The UART digital inventory also needs independent native pin/join enforcement. The unbuilt board's UART loading/timing, return/bypass behavior, resistor/probe access, stack/process and electrical qualification remain open. Independent Gate A remains mandatory before fabrication.

The source-membership check identifies these seven RAW tracks as original 215-edge copper, not later additions. This ADR explicitly corrects that initial classification before any trial retirement. The existing six original single-track replacement entries remain unchanged. This new ignored chain scope preserves the exact retained external contact inventory on In4; internal vertices with no retained external contact may change. It grants no permission to retire any other original digital chain. Canonical adoption would need a separate independently enforced chain policy; the current checker continues to reject it.

## 62-edge candidate and revised local length screen

A direct R47.2 B/In4 microvia removes the disrupted rear escape. Replacing the seven RAW tracks with 33 full-width In4 tracks, without new horizontal B/In2 ground-plane runs, restores every old physical group and reaches 62 opens. It preserves all five actual retained source-layer contacts. Three refills retain 62, full native DRC remains exactly the same 104 findings, and restoring the same three orphan vias adds only their original dangling findings with all groups/counts unchanged.

The calculated RAW replacement is 5.039244 mm versus 1.150000 mm retired. It exceeds the initial retired-plus-3-mm planning screen; revise only this exact chain's prospective bound to retired plus **4 mm**, or 5.15 mm total. This is a local geometry-study bound, not a current or timing rating. With assumed 18 µm copper and 1.724e-8 Ω·m at 20 °C, the additional 3.889244 mm of 0.20 mm trace calculates to about 0.0186 Ω. The unchanged 499 Ω series resistor remains in circuit; no driver, load or current setting changes. Actual copper, probe loading, edge rate/return, partial-power and electrical qualification remain open. No other chain or length bound changes.

Before canonical selection, freeze all 63-edge additions and USB status metadata. Independently pin the seven originals, 33 replacement signatures, five source-layer contacts and two exact interfaces; reject a catalog that changes any of them. Extend only the exact UART0_TX digital inventory under ADR 0047 and keep the 120 mm hard bound, 0.20 mm other-net clearance and unchanged adjacent microvia policy. Complete the full replay/fault/native/source acceptance gate before selecting either inventory.

## Selected outcome

The [62-edge evidence](../development/led-routing-progress-62-study-2026-10-28.md) passes the complete gate: stable native/physical counts, unchanged full 104 findings, all frozen 63-edge additions/groups/poses, two exact interfaces and five retained source contacts, identical replay, 50 native/99 portable guards, canonical/source/256 LED checks and unchanged three-orphan restoration. Select only the pinned seven-original/33-replacement chain and the separately pinned UART digital inventory. The calculated local replacement region is x = 50.25–52.35 mm, y = 7.75–9.00 mm. All other source copper remains protected subject to the preceding six exact replacements and three orphan proofs. No general source-chain or added-copper deletion, ground retirement, additional width/clearance relaxation or fabrication permission follows.
