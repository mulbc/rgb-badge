# ADR 0247: R9 front supported gate discovery

Date: 2026-11-20
Status: Accepted for bounded read-only discovery.

The three ADR0246 corridor screens each sample5,324 poses and find no
complete placement (5,082 courtyard/242 pad failures per part). Preserve all
source selectors, row vias and existing package poses on that evidence.

Use the immutable23-open source. Independently study only exact R9 on F at
(80.600,8.200) mm /0°, using native top-bottom flip and the same local
ERJ2RKF1001X package, fields and numbered nets. Screen ordinary0.20 mm F
supply from its actual pin1 to original main VLED port(79.100,7.800).
Screen F gate pin2 to proposed top access(81.500,8.200).

Study supported0.30/0.10 mm B/In4 plus In3/In4 accesses at actual retained
PAIR04 gate body(79.750,9.000), and F/In1 plus In1/In2 accesses at the top
point. Use only a0.50/0.30 mm mechanical buried In2/In3 centre, sampled at
0.050 mm increments in x=79.000–82.000 /y=7.000–10.000. Screen ordinary
In3 root-to-centre and In2 centre-to-top leads, complete gate conductor≤12 mm
and supply≤6 mm. Require exact native source/new mutual copper, actual F
courtyards, every source/new hole and unchanged0.25 mm drill policy. No
aligned mechanical-to-micro hole exception is allowed.

Catalogue the four original same-position R9 supply microvias (B/In4,
In3/In4, historical unsupported In2/In3 and In1/In2 at32.750,11.750).
They are prospective private-leaf retirements only; prove their complete
outside contacts before any later freeze. Keep every other source copper
item and every previous group. Write no PCB. A clear sample requires a
separate exact frozen prototype plus all22-open/native/physical, source/
identity, exact104, replay/inverse/fault/visual gates before selection.
Current/default-off/protection and timing/return/thermal, trial package,
process/controlled-stack, fit and independent Gate A/fabrication holds persist.

The first exact sample is rejected: R11 courtyard, original LED_SIN F
conductors, COL_05_B/R In4 at the gate body, and COL_11_G/COL_13_B inner
conductors block its fixed objects. Its four proposed microvia holes clear,
but no centre is sampled through those failed prerequisites.

Refine to independent finite native port discovery. Screen R9 front poses
x=78.000–82.000 /y=6.000–12.000 mm at0.050 mm increments and four
cardinal orientations. Require actual F courtyards/pads and direct or either
one-bend ordinary supply leads≤6 mm to each of three witnessed retained
F main VLED points: (79.100,7.800), (78.400,7.800), (79.075,4.550).

Separately sample supported B/In4+In3/In4 gate captures at0.050 mm in
x=78.000–82.000 /y=7.500–10.500 mm, with a≤3 mm ordinary B lead to
an actual PAIR04 gate pad. Check both Cu spans, source/new drill policy
and actual source port contact. No copper retires; old R9 leaf contacts
are only catalogued. This independent port inventory cannot claim a full
gate or reduced open count. Any subsequent linked search needs every
fixed prerequisite, source/new mutual and complete successor gates.

The4941-point aligned lower-pair inventory fails native copper at every
point. Preserve that result. Extend only the lower-pair capture search to
x=75.000–85.000 /y=6.000–13.000 mm at0.050 mm increments, with a≤12 mm
ordinary B lead to the same actual gate pads. Keep all source/new Cu and
hole rules. This is another bounded read-only port inventory, not a PCB or
part migration. No conclusion about staggered transitions is established.

The expanded28,341-point aligned lower search has28,326 capture-Cu failures
and15 direct B lead failures; no selected access. The front supply-only
screen finds118 complete supply poses among39,204 (19,160 courtyard,
18,670 pads,1,256 supply-lead failures). Record those separately: they are
not complete gate joins. Recheck the15 source-Cu-clear lower pairs with
direct and both orthogonal one-bend B lead templates; keep every original
Cu/pad and full new/source drill rule. Retain all Cu-clear candidate coordinates
and exact source blockers even if those templates fail. No PCB is authorized.

All15 Cu-clear lower pairs pass the full source/new drill check, but direct
and one-bend B leads still fail. Discover a B-only ordinary0.20 mm path from
the three actual existing PAIR04 gate pads to those exact pairs, within
x=75.000–85.000 /y=6.000–13.000 mm at0.025 mm eight-neighbour steps,
≤100,000 expanded nodes and≤12 mm conductor. Native source Cu/pad checks
apply to points, edges and merged segments. Keep all source copper and
holes. No reduced open count or complete gate fanout follows from this
lower B lead alone. Write no PCB; preserve all original successor holds.
