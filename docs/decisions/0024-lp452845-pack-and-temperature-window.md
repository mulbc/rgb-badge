# ADR 0024: LP452845 design pack and hardware charge-temperature window

- Date: 2026-10-09
- Status: Accepted as the Rev A design target for capture; no battery or board has been qualified or released
- Relates to: ADR 0019, CHG-001/002/003, SAF-001/002 and power hold H1/H3

## Decision

Design the Rev A battery interface around LiPol's protected, two-wire **LP452845, drawing FD_6225_10 index 1**, terminated with JST `PHR-2` and AWG 26 leads. Use JST `S2B-PH-SM4-TB` as the mating board-header target. This supersedes the bare-wire `LP503055` as the working pack for schematic and fit work; do not substitute an unnamed LP452845 or generic PH pack. The assembly drawing gives a 47 × 28.5 × 4.8 mm maximum body, 900 mAh minimum capacity, 450 mA maximum charge, 900 mA *maximum* discharge, 4.20 V ±50 mV maximum charge voltage, and +10 to +45 °C charge range. Its drawing does not call the 900 mA discharge rating continuous and does not establish present stock. The nominal runtime estimate is 6.29 h at the stated reference workload, before measured variation.

Keep a battery-facing PCB NTC connected directly to BQ24074 `TS`, as ADR 0019 requires. Use exact Murata `NCU15XH103F60RC` (10 kΩ ±1%, 0402, B25/50 3380 K ±1%) for this first capture. The charger's native NTC window is approximately 0–50 °C with this curve; it **cannot alone qualify** the chosen pack's tighter +10 to +45 °C limits. Do not add a fixed TS bypass resistor.

For the BQ24074 ISET capture, select exact Panasonic **`ERA2AEB2491X`**, 2.49 kΩ, 0.1% initial tolerance and ±25 ppm/K TCR, using the existing audited project-local ERA2 0402 land. Retain the conservative **±1% total programmed-resistance envelope** (initial, temperature, assembly and service drift) for the 317–396 mA charge-current calculation against this pack's 450 mA maximum. This is a selected source part for the forthcoming charger sheet, not a populated or approved fabrication BOM line. The maximum current, pack thermal behavior and any ITERM/TMR settings still require review and coupon measurement. [Panasonic exact-part specification](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/high-precision-chip-resistors/models/ERA2AEB2491X).

Add exact TI `TMP390A2DRLR` at the battery-facing PCB location beside the NTC. Power it from the protected USB 3.3 V logic rail. Set its independent hot trip to 40 °C with Panasonic `ERJ2RKF2151X` (2.15 kΩ ±1%) on `SETA` pin 1, and cold trip to 15 °C with `ERJ2RKF1402X` (14.0 kΩ ±1%) on `SETB` pin 2. The latter selects 5 °C hysteresis, so the nominal reset points are 35 and 20 °C. Tie the two open-drain, active-low outputs (hot `OUTA` pin 6; cold `OUTB` pin 4) to a `TEMP_OK` node with an exact Panasonic `ERJ2RKF1002X` 10 kΩ pull-up to the same USB 3.3 V rail. Drive the active-low OE of a **second** exact `SN74AUP1G125DBVR` with `TEMP_OK`; tie its A input low and connect its three-state Y output to the E1 charger-gate EN node. A temperature fault therefore actively sinks EN, while both healthy outputs leave this gate high-impedance. This uses the already-audited AUP part and does not alter the proposed 220 kΩ EN pull-up or its independent passive pull-down, supervisor and source-permission inhibits. Place exact Murata `GRM155R71C104KA88D` 0.1 µF bypass capacitors at the TMP390 and second AUP supply pins; TMP390 pin 3 is ground. The logic is hardware-only and also works with the badge's physical switch OFF. BQ `TS` and its safety timer remain active as separate safeguards.

## Why and limits

TI gives the BQ24074 `TS` 270–330 mV hot and 2.0–2.2 V cold thresholds with a 72–78 µA bias. Its standard 10 kΩ curve produces a wider window, and TI states the usual series/parallel network can only **extend** that window. TI's TMP390A2 has ±1.5 °C trip accuracy at 0–70 °C and 2.5–5.5 V, plus 0.5 s sampling. The 15/40 °C settings leave a nominal 5 °C guard band to either pack limit. After the ±1.5 °C sensor error, the *remaining allowable cell-to-board difference is 3.5 °C* at each boundary. This is an acceptance target for physical testing, not an observed offset. Heating rate, sample period, propagation, and location require the same cell-surface comparison in ADR 0019; if that bound fails, change the physical sensing arrangement before final approval.

TI specifies 1–10 kΩ output pull-ups for TMP390; the originally proposed direct connection to the 220 kΩ EN pull-up fell outside that range. The `TEMP_OK` buffer corrects this. With two outputs at their specified ±0.1 µA high-state leakage, the 10 kΩ pull-up loses at most 2 mV from those two paths alone; the AUP input load and low-voltage logic thresholds still need a full corner check. In the fault state a 3.6 V rail drives at most 0.36 mA through the 10 kΩ pull-up, below TMP390's 3 mA specified low-output test load. The AUP output, rather than TMP390, joins the EN leakage/sink budget; do **not** reuse the earlier 2.25 V EN estimate as a proof for this revised circuit. The supervisor's proposed fixed delay is 180–420 ms; TMP390 POR, resistor sampling, output validity, open/short resistor faults, and rail loss still require a connected-circuit review. Keep the temperature guard on E1 EN so no charge input is admitted during its asserted faults; do not let application firmware override it.

The exact header footprint, mating orientation, pack polarity, lead bend, 900 mA discharge duty, 450 mA charge limit with total ISET error, stock and thermal behavior are required before BOM freeze. No battery has been purchased or physically tested. Independent Gate A review remains required before fabrication.
