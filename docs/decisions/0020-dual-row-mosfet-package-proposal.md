# ADR 0020: Proposed complementary MOSFET package for each row stage

- Date: 2026-10-06
- Status: **Proposed for electrical and manufacturing review; no schematic/BOM substitution**
- Applies to: coupon row-stage validation and the eventual 48 × 16 final badge
- Retains: 16 individually selected common-anode rows, hardware-default row blanking, VLED fault inhibition, 1.95 mm pixel pitch and independent Gate A

## Problem and proposed direction

The existing row circuit uses 16 `DMP2066LSN-7` P-channel MOSFETs and 16 `2N7002K-7` N-channel gate sinks. Their project-local courtyards consume **397.6 mm²** outside the projected pack. A [connector-first placement trial](../../mechanical/connector-first-trial-2026-10-06.md) cannot fit four of them plus seven other captured parts, before adding the unfinished power circuit.

Evaluate `DMC1229UFDB-7` (Diodes Incorporated, U-DFN2020-6 Type B) as one **electrically separate N/P pair per row**, retaining the same 1 kΩ P-gate pull-up, 100 kΩ N-gate pull-down, decoder and row-control truth table. The N channel would sink its own P-gate node; the P channel would switch VLED to its row. No transistor connection is internal except each device's own terminals, so schematic pin mapping and external gate wiring still require a fresh source audit. This is a candidate, not approval to edit the canonical row sheet.

The manufacturer's [DMC1229UFDB datasheet](https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/DMC1229UFDB.pdf) gives a 2.00 mm nominal body, 0.605 mm maximum body height, ±8 V VGS limits and 12 V drain-source ratings. At 25 °C it specifies N-channel RDS(on) ≤34 mΩ at 2.5 V gate drive, and P-channel RDS(on) ≤81 mΩ at −2.5 V. It lists P-channel steady-state ID −3.0 A at 70 °C **on a 1 × 1 inch, high-coverage 2 oz test board**; that is not a badge-board thermal rating. The exact orderable suffix is `-7` for the stated tape-and-reel option; availability and assembler handling are unverified.

## Quantified screening result

The [driver calculation](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/driver-capture.md) gives 233.02 mA for one 16-pixel white coupon row at maximum nominal settings. Scaling the same per-pixel setting to 48 pixels gives **699.06 mA per active final-badge row** as a calculation, not a board measurement. With 81 mΩ, `I²R` would be about **39.6 mW while that row is on** at the stated 25 °C resistance condition. It is not a maximum hot-device/assembled-board dissipation bound; pulse edges, LED settings, copper, temperature and simultaneous-row faults require review. A 1 kΩ pull-up from nominal 3.944 V VLED would ask the N channel to sink approximately 3.94 mA while the row is selected; its 3.3 V gate drive lies above the datasheet's 2.5 V RDS(on) test level. Verify actual drain-low voltage, timing and reset behavior rather than inferring them from VGS(th).

The [first-author candidate footprint](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/dual-row-package-library-audit.md) has a 3 × 3 mm courtyard derived from the manufacturer's suggested Type B lands. Sixteen candidate packages consume **144.0 mm²**, nominally **253.6 mm² less** than the 32 existing courtyards. The updated [dual-package XY screen](../../mechanical/review/dual-row-package-plan.svg) moves the decoder to the upper right and groups the staged VLED converter in an 11 × 9.3 mm lower-right bay. It gives all currently captured non-row parts, 16 pair boxes, the connector pocket, BQ24074/TPS63020/two TPS259474 **IC-package minima**, and twelve VLED passives nonoverlapping positions. [ADR 0021](0021-external-battery-current-measurement.md) removes the on-board INA232/shunt; including its historical IC box in the same first-fit order leaves one of the sixteen pair boxes unplaced. This is a heuristic comparison, not proof that a monitor cannot fit with a different placement. The drawing has no routed switching loop or routing gap and excludes remaining charger/input support, gauge buffer, hardware display interlock, mounting, RF cable and copper. It does **not** prove full fit.

The 0.605 mm package height cannot be credited automatically beneath the battery: the existing 11 mm section already leaves only 0.25 mm nominal with a 0.55 mm 0402. The pair packages stay outside the pack in this screen. Their manufacturer land pattern now has a first-author transcription, but its pad numbering, stencil and assembly use still need independent review.

## Capture and review conditions

1. Calculate a bounded per-row current including LED-current tolerance, fault/overlap behavior and the chosen VLED rail; check P-channel SOA, hot RDS(on), gate-source/transient margins and copper temperature on the compact board. The 25 °C arithmetic above is only a candidate screen.
2. Audit the current manufacturer bottom-view pin map, pad geometry, pin-one mark, stencil and recommended land pattern into a project-local symbol/footprint. Do not replace 32 schematic instances by reference-number coincidence or treat the trial boxes as lands.
3. Preserve all hardware-default OFF, reset, boot and programming blanking behavior and verify row dead time, turn-off and visible ghosting. The existing 2N7002K uncertainty at 3.3 V does not by itself validate the replacement.
4. Decide whether the 16 × 16 coupon should use the pair package so its measurements validate the final row stage. Record that decision before schematic capture; a coupon with the old stage cannot qualify the new stage by analogy.
5. Run native KiCad ERC/netlist/visual checks, real PCB DRC, assembly/thermal review and independent Gate A before fabrication. No current, charge or enclosure limit changes through this proposal.

This is the highest-leverage component-area option found in the connector-first screen. It is not an accepted design change or a PCB release.

**2026-10-11 follow-on:** The [current-inventory LP452845 screen](../../mechanical/current-lp452845-xy-screen-2026-10-11.md) reads the 466-item coupon export, screens its rear non-LED courtyards and projects three final driver bodies. It finds 16–18 unplaced boxes with the canonical row parts, versus zero with sixteen *geometric* dual-package substitutions in two greedy orders. Adding even an empty 7 × 12 mm interlock reservation makes the dual variant leave one or two packages unplaced in those orders, so zero-unplaced has no demonstrated safety-circuit margin. The [electrical decision screen](../design/dual-row-electrical-screen-2026-10-11.md) finds nominal P-channel loss manageable as an initial calculation, while exact numeric pad sequence, compact-board hot behavior and real row-current/edge limits remain open. Status stays proposed; the canonical schematic is unchanged.

A 108 mm trial PCB, extending the 106 mm board on its right edge, clears the remaining area-first courtyard miss with that reserved interlock pocket. The 110 mm outer-case target does not yet accommodate confirmed walls or USB cutout at this PCB size. No board or case dimension is accepted by this geometric screen.

**2026-10-12 captured-interlock follow-on:** The [497-item courtyard screen](../../mechanical/captured-interlock-fit-2026-10-12.md) now includes the nine interlock ICs, twenty support parts and two candidate test pads. It leaves 20 greedy misses at 108 mm with the canonical row FETs and zero with the geometric dual-package substitution. In the zero-miss trial, all nine bypass capacitors land 19.06–49.66 mm from their ICs. A second 3 mm bypass-locality trial leaves three capacitors unplaced at 108 mm and one at 110 mm with the paired-row geometry. The result strengthens the case for exploring the pair package but shows that first-fit occupancy alone is not a usable layout. Exact numeric pad mapping, compact-board current/heat, grouped routing and assembly review still precede any schematic replacement.

**2026-10-14 grouped-power XY follow-on:** A [new 110 mm candidate screen](../../mechanical/paired-charger-110mm-screen-2026-10-14.md) finds one zero-miss first-fit ordering for all current coupon rear parts, the sixteen proposed row-pair boxes, two extra projected final drivers, and locally reserved E0/E1/BQ support groups including C53/C54/R92. **2026-10-14 adopted-length follow-on:** After the owner accepted a 114 mm working PCB target under ADR 0033, the [corrected native placement study](../../docs/development/paired-charger-114mm-placement-study-2026-10-14.md) fixed TH1 and U39 centrally under the projected pack and grouped the charger. Its [topology comparison](../../mechanical/review/paired-charger-114mm-topology.json) leaves 15 canonical-row boxes unplaced in unconstrained greedy first-fit, 17 with nearby interlock bypass capacitors, and zero for the sixteen geometric pair substitutions in both runs. This remains order-dependent XY evidence, not proof that separate FETs cannot fit or permission to capture an unaudited pair pin map. The pair's intrinsic 0.15 mm pad gaps also exceed the current 0.20 mm clearance rule; assembly/process review remains necessary.

**2026-10-14 numbered-pin alternative:** The exact onsemi `NTLJD3119CTBG` is another 2 × 2 mm complementary N/P pair. Its [manufacturer datasheet, NTLJD3119C/D Rev. 6](https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/ntljd3119c-d.pdf), page 1, explicitly numbers its **top-view** terminals: 1 = N source S1, 2 = N gate G1, 3 = P drain D2, 4 = P source S2, 5 = P gate G2, 6 = N drain D1. The two exposed drain islands are separately labelled D2 and D1. This resolves the *numeric-map documentation problem for this alternate part only*; it does not verify the Diodes map or make the two footprints interchangeable. The onsemi WDFN6 CASE 506AN drawing on page 10 recommends a solder-mask-defined land pattern and gives 0.80 mm maximum height, versus the Diodes part's 0.605 mm. A separate exact footprint, stencil/process review and under-pack exclusion are required before use.

At 2.5 V gate drive the onsemi datasheet gives maximum 85 mΩ N and 135 mΩ P, versus 34/81 mΩ for the Diodes candidate. At the existing **calculated** 699.06 mA final-row current, the onsemi P-channel's 25 °C, −2.5 V tabulated maximum gives approximately **66 mW** `I²R` while continuously on, versus 39.6 mW for the Diodes comparison. This is not a hot-device, assembled-board or fault bound. The [LCSC listing](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C274622.html) showed 175 orderable pieces on 2026-10-14, enough for a small prototype but not a stock commitment. This part is a credible way to continue a numbered-pin library and fit trial if the Diodes source remains unresolved; **no schematic, BOM or placement substitution is authorized by this note**.
