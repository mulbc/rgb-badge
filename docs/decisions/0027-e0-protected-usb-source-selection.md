# ADR 0027: Select the provisional E0 protected-USB source

Date: 2026-10-11. Status: selected and provisionally captured; fault and operating qualification open.

Place exact `TPS259474ARPWR` E0 between connector `VBUS_CONNECTOR` and the existing `+5V_USB` logic supply. Feed E1 IN from that same protected output. E0 must start autonomously from the connector with E1 still disabled; no application firmware, Type-C advertisement or physical ON signal controls E0. Remove the nonphysical `+5V_USB` and E1 protected-input source markers only when the native source bridge is present. Retain the connector-facing test pad and all USB ESD/CC/data nets.

Use the selected exact `ERJ2RKF3321X` 3.32 kΩ from E0 ILM to ground, with ITIMER open for the fastest circuit-breaker response. This keeps E0 at TI's published **0.850–1.150 A device test point** rather than raising its limit. The captured BQ24074 fixed input setting has a calculated 0.361–0.476 A interval, leaving 0.374 A between its high corner and the *nominal-resistor* E0 minimum test point before USB logic, capacitor charging and resistor drift. This is a DC coordination screen, not a guarantee of either whole-port compliance or fault energy. Preserve E1's own ILM and BQ's lower hardware ILIM independently.

Use exact `ERJ2RKF3832X` 38.3 kΩ from raw VBUS to E0 OVLO and exact `ERJ2RKF1002X` 10 kΩ to ground. The earlier DC screen gives 5.620–6.006 V rising and 5.112–5.481 V falling over the specified threshold, ±1% resistor and OVLO leakage corners. Normal 5.5 V is just below the minimum rising threshold in that screen; ripple, resistor temperature drift, overshoot and fault-disconnect delay remain unqualified. The acceptable behavior after an overvoltage trip is to require the source to fall below the recovery threshold or be detached. Do not interpret OVLO as a surge clamp.

Drive E0 EN/UVLO from raw VBUS through exact `ERJ2RKF3903X` 390 kΩ, which exceeds TI's 350 kΩ minimum recommended pull-up resistance for inputs above 5 V or reverse-polarity exposure. The resistor provides an autonomous enable without a firmware path. Confirm pin voltage/current, attach and reverse-fault behavior in the connected review; the device's internal 2.44–2.64 V input UVP is not a USB-valid qualification threshold. Leave E0 PG/PGTH explicitly unused for this first capture; existing independent VBUS and logic supervision continue to determine E1 permission.

Select exact TDK `C1608X7R1H105K080AB` 1 µF/50 V X7R at E0 IN, exact Murata `GRM187R61A226ME15` 22 µF/10 V X5R close to E0 OUT, and exact Murata `GRM1555C1H222GA01D` 2.2 nF/50 V C0G from dVdt to ground. The TDK 0603 land must be checked against its manufacturer drawing before capture; a Murata-named footprint cannot be silently assigned to a TDK part. E0's IN node can see connector faults while its OUT may retain energy. Actual effective capacitance, cable-inductance transients, controlled slew/inrush and layout remain unqualified. Maintain the existing USB connector electrical envelope and do not raise any USB, battery-charge, LED or regulator current limit.

Sources: [TI TPS25947 Rev C](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps25947.pdf), [Panasonic 38.3 kΩ](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/general-purpose-chip-resistors/models/ERJ2RKF3832X), [Panasonic 390 kΩ](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/general-purpose-chip-resistors/models/ERJ2RKF3903X), [Panasonic 10 kΩ](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/general-purpose-chip-resistors/models/ERJ2RKF1002X), [TDK 1 µF/50 V](https://product.tdk.com/en/search/capacitor/ceramic/mlcc/info?part_no=C1608X7R1H105K080AB), and the [existing protection screen](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/usb-input-protection-screening.md).
