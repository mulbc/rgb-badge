# ADR 0032: Require a heartbeat and a distinct display-arm edge

Date: 2026-10-12. Status: compact package set selected for provisional library capture; functional circuit remains proposed and unqualified.

Use two reset-dominant hardware flip-flops for the display permission. The first remembers a dedicated watchdog heartbeat after the application rail and controller are ready. The second may arm only on a separate rising `DISPLAY_ARM_CLK` edge while the first is set. A common asynchronous clear removes both permissions on application-rail supervision failure, `ESP_EN` reset, mode-button press, or withdrawal of the firmware `DISPLAY_ENABLE` request. The independent watchdog also clears both; its output returning high does not recreate either clock edge. GPIO15/module pad 8 is the heartbeat and GPIO16/module pad 9 is the arm pulse, subject to the exact module-library audit. The firmware must issue the heartbeat before a deliberate arm pulse and must not pulse arm in programming mode.

The converter enable is the armed state **and** the live `DISPLAY_ENABLE` request, with the existing 100 kΩ pull-down retained. Decoder E is high unless the arm is set, VLED is inside a measured-ready voltage window, and GPIO8 requests a row. `ROW_ENABLE_REQ_N` and `ROW_ENABLE_N` stay distinct. The TPS63020 PG output is not the voltage qualification. The row output must be forced high when GPIO8 is stuck low and any permission is removed. This implements the [interface contract](../design/display-interlock-interface-2026-10-05.md) without raising a current limit or changing the accepted row topology.

Add an exact Panasonic `ERJ2RKF4701X` 4.7 kΩ passive VLED bleed in the proposed circuit. Its nominal 0.839 mA operating draw and 0.947 s ideal 88 µF discharge to 0.4 V are calculations; actual retained rail energy and row conduction must be measured. The [pin-level proposal](../design/display-interlock-circuit-proposal-2026-10-12.md) lists the full first support-part schedule. This value and the isolated logic are now captured in source; no display output is enabled.

The [pin-level proposal](../design/display-interlock-circuit-proposal-2026-10-12.md) names the candidate orderable ICs and the signal/state equations. It is a design input, **not approval to connect VLED EN**. The output-isolated capture passed native ERC/XML/PDF review, but exact clear-net timing, threshold and propagation corners, VLED discharge, grouped physical placement and software-reset GPIO defaults remain unqualified. Gate A and coupon fault-injection measurements remain mandatory before fabrication or a claim of safe operation.

For provisional firmware design, use a 1 ms heartbeat HIGH pulse every 100 ms from the playback-progress path, with no more than 250 ms between falling edges, and delay the first separate arm pulse at least 2 ms after a qualifying falling edge. These are [calculated timing candidates](../design/display-interlock-circuit-proposal-2026-10-12.md), not hardware-verified limits; the exact watchdog timeout, GPIO drive under its 1 kΩ bias, and reset/clear timing remain acceptance work.

**2026-10-12 source increment:** The [output-isolated interlock capture](../development/display-interlock-capture-review-2026-10-12.md) is now root-linked as page 20. GPIO15/16 and all proposed logic/support parts are connected in KiCad; the two resulting VLED/row outputs terminate at test pads and do not drive the converter or decoder. Zero native ERC findings and complete pin/net checks verify source connectivity only. This is not acceptance of the interlock, a board-width decision, or permission to connect the display outputs before the remaining electrical and physical review.

The [captured-courtyard fit screen](../../mechanical/captured-interlock-fit-2026-10-12.md) includes all 31 new parts. At 108 mm its greedy placement misses 20 boxes with the canonical 32 row FETs, while the unselected dual-package row trial misses none. In the latter trial, the nine interlock bypass capacitors lie 19.06–49.66 mm from their ICs. Requiring a trial 3 mm gap leaves three bypass capacitors unplaced at 108 mm. Neither result is a grouped circuit placement or evidence of a routable board.

The [first IC-body fit screen](../../mechanical/display-interlock-body-fit-2026-10-12.md) finds 1–6 unplaced boxes at 106 mm across two arbitrary body allowances and no robust 108 mm result with the larger allowance. It floors the supervisor box to the existing audited local courtyard, but otherwise excludes support passives and does not authorize a wider board or case. Actual footprints and a grouped placement are the next fit gate.
The same screen tries smaller orderable logic packages without changing function: 108 mm then clears both **IC-body-only** allowances, while 106 mm clears only the optimistic one. Sub-0.5 mm assembly lands, passives and case walls remain to be qualified.
An additional twenty-0402 stress allowance outside the pack still gives zero greedy misses at 108 mm with the compact bodies, but it has no circuit-locality constraint and is not a captured support-part count. Keep 108 mm as a placement hypothesis, not an approved board dimension.

Select that exact compact logic package set for **provisional library and schematic-source work**: two `SN74LVC1G74RSER`, one `SN74LVC1G04DSFR`, one `SN74LVC1G11DRYR` and one `SN74LVC1G08DSFR`; keep `TPS3823A-33DBVR`, `TLV6700DSER`, `SN74LVC2G00DCUR` and `SN74LVC2G08DCUR`. This changes packages, not logic or electrical limits. The two RSE flip-flops reuse the existing local RSE0008A land in a [new exact symbol](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/display-interlock-library-audit.md) whose pin map passed source audit. The DSF, DCU, DSE and DRY lands and all eight exact IC symbols have passed source-geometry, 53-pin and native-export checks. The DSE footprint follows TI's newer 4220552/B spacing; its mask choice and the tiny DRY stencil need assembler approval. Leadless-pad assembly capability, supply availability, grouped placement and the 108 mm enclosure section are still open. Do not connect converter EN or declare the interlock complete merely because its libraries render.
