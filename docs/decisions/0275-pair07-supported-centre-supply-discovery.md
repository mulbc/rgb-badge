# ADR 0275: PAIR07 supported centre supply discovery

Date: 2026-11-20
Status: Accepted for bounded read-only additive route discovery.

ADR0271 PAIR07 exhausts18831 B/In4/In3 states without a join. Keep all
parts and all19367 selected22-open source Cu fixed. Extend that same
x70..88/y11..21 mm,25 mm/400000-state/0.025 mm graph monotonically to
In2 using at most one additional supported mechanical buried In2/In3
via0.50/0.30 mm. Lower B/In4 and In4/In3 captures remain supported
0.30/0.10 mm microvias, at most three new transitions overall. No new
centre microvia, through via, width, hole, clearance or profile change.
Targets are actual original main VLED39 conductors on these layers.

All new vias must clear source Cu and the full source hole policy.
Mutual projected close holes are allowed only for supported disjoint
spans, or exact same-net aligned adjacent outer microvias. A centre
mechanical via sharing a span needs the actual0.25 mm hole clearance;
no centre/microvia alignment exception. A complete candidate must refill
at21 matching opens/VLED40/GND197/USB32, every210 old pad group/all19367
attached source Cu and zero orphans. Final full source/new hole audit
remains mandatory. No PCB is written or selected. Electrical/return/
trial-package/stack/process/fit and independent Gate A holds persist;
fabrication is prohibited.
