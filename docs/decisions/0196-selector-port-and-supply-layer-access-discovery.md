# ADR 0196: Selector port and supply layer access discovery

Date: 2026-11-16
Status: Accepted for read-only complete-interface discovery only.

Both ADR0195 complete trials remain at 29 opens and are rejected. The first
supported supply capture cannot reach In3 because original selector-eight
wire 401b8b44-9f6a-5db2-9684-00f87af760dd occupies its landing. Read-only
freeze that exact 0.20 mm In3 wire (56.900,17.650)–(57.850,17.650) mm and
its full native source contacts, in addition to the unchanged 80-item private
selector cohort. Keep every other source item and all parts/poses fixed.

Screen the first or second exact ADR0194 ordinary B supply escape with supported
aligned B/In4 and In3/In4 captures at (56.900,17.600) or (56.900,17.650) mm.
The second lies beyond the actual ROW_08_A diagonal obstruction; native
screening must establish this clearance rather than infer it from the first. Screen a separate
supported aligned selector capture at x=57.950–58.100 / y=17.600–17.700 mm,
0.050 mm samples, retaining physical contact with every original outside
In3 wire beyond the retired 0.950 mm leaf. Include actual foreign/no-net
copper, original edge, full-board holes and mutual new copper. Write no PCB.

A clear pair is incomplete until both the full USB join and original selector
group are restored under separate exact prototype scope. All 216 original
groups/interfaces, matching reduced native/physical count, exact104 and every
source/replay/inverse/contact/hole/orphan/fault/visual gate remain mandatory.
No electrical/thermal/current/protection/bypass/stack/process/fit/Gate A or
fabrication hold changes.
