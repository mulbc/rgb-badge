# ADR 0220: Row-zero outer colour hop discovery

Date: 2026-11-19
Status: Accepted for read-only native discovery only.

ADR0219 exposes thirteen mutually clear supported gate gateways after
prospectively omitting the one frozen COL_09_R In4 wire. The whole-wire
In3 graph exhausts at 1,573 nodes, and its bounded local In3 endpoint screen
finds no capture pair. No colour replacement or source successor is selected.

Separately discover a B colour hop for the same exact one-wire cohort and
unchanged two original far In3/In4 via interfaces. Keep fixed gate access at
(74.825,3.250) mm with its supported B/In4 and In3/In4 pair. Retain original
In4 end portions as exact ordinary replacements to two new supported B/In4
colour captures on y=3.250 mm. Sample left x=73.000–74.250 mm and right
x=76.750–78.500 mm at 0.050 mm centres. All other parts/copper/rules remain
fixed. Screen ordinary 0.20 mm B orthogonal detours, then eight-neighbour
0.050 mm refinement if necessary, within x=73.000–78.500 /
y=0.750–5.750 mm and at most 8 mm B hop length.

Require all source/mutual capture/track and full-board hole checks. Add no
other prospective retirement, unsupported transition, smaller feature or
part/value/current/function change. Write no PCB. A possible B hop must
still prove every old group, particularly the full original ground group,
after native refill, complete colour and gate restoration, matching reduced
counts, exact104 and every full successor source/replay/inverse/fault/visual
gate under a separate frozen prototype scope. All qualification/Gate A and
fabrication holds remain unchanged; no electrical return approval is inferred.
