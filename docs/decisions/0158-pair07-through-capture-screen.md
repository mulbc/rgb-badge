# ADR 0158: Read-only PAIR07 full-span capture alternative

Date: 2026-11-15
Status: Accepted for read-only native geometry discovery only.

ADR 0157's prepared outer stack preserves every source group but the supported
ordinary grid search exhausts its 1,215 sampled points without a join. Keep
that unfinished prototype unselected. Revisit only PAIR07.4 VLED on the exact
immutable selected 29-open source; do not use its prepared captures as input.

Read-only screen a provisional 0.50/0.30 mm full-span F/B plated-through via
(as previously scoped in ADR 0092), with centres at 0.025 mm offsets within
±0.500 mm of the actual fixed S2 pad. Require positive native pad-copper contact,
check actual foreign/no-net/mechanical copper on all six layers, every source
hole at the full unchanged 0.25 mm clearance and the board/cutout edges with
0.50 mm copper-edge allowance. No disjoint-hole exception applies to a full
span. Preserve literal `NTLJD3119CTBG_TRIAL_ONLY` and all fields/provisional
status, every other source part/copper/net/profile/rule and safety boundary.

Record actual individually filled VLED plane components connected to the main
source group and physically touched by each clear capture. A clear through
capture or a plane name alone is not a selected join. Write no PCB. Freeze any
actual successful capture and witness scope before implementation; all 217
source groups and full native/physical/finding/contact/replay/inverse/orphan/
fault/source/visual gates remain mandatory. No unsupported centre microvia,
current/voltage increase or protection/default-off change. Through drilling,
via-in-pad filling, current capacity and the controlled stack remain explicitly
unqualified. All electrical/part/thermal/process/fit/Gate A and fabrication
holds remain; zero opens is unfinished.
