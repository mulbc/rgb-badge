# ADR 0182: Complete sensor with verified ground-plane substitution

Date: 2026-11-16
Status: Accepted for ignored complete-circuit feasibility only; no successor selected.

ADR0181's exact seventeen-item GND bridge removal preserves all 216 original
groups, 28 matching native/physical opens, exact original 104 findings and both
actual B/In2 endpoint-plane contacts with retained witnesses. Three native
fills are stable; restoring all seventeen source items/types reproduces the
selected parent byte for byte with all groups and nine zone fills. The basis
remains unselected and electrically/thermally unqualified.

Combine only that verified GND substitution with the exact ADR0179 two-part
sensor prototype on immutable 28-open source. Retire the disjoint union of
seventeen original sensor-local B tracks and seventeen original GND bridge
items (34 total); freeze complete native original source contacts and exact
UUID/signature/type ledgers. No other source item or original via may change.
Keep exact U39/R90 poses, R89 and all other parts fixed, and the exact ADR0179
ordinary VDD neck/B-In4 capture. Preserve all thermal/programming/bypass,
part/field/local-pad, profile, layer/width and safety bounds.

Use only ADR0179's original five restoration nets, local window/cardinal grid,
500,000-node and per-join/per-net length budgets. The newly freed corridor may
be used by ordinary supported copper; no extra row-bus retirement, new rule or
centre microvia is authorized. Reject every split of current groups after each
new join. The preparatory prototype may lose only the intended hot/cold/output
connections, never a different original group. Full source-ground interfaces
from both cohorts remain required after final refill.

Selection remains forbidden until all 216 original groups, complete local
programming/output/ground/bypass/supply, every original outside copper/pad/
plane contact, matching native/physical reduction below 28 and exact original
104 findings pass together. Full independent source/contact/hole/replay/inverse/
orphan/fault/visual qualification and separate selection decision are required.
Removing one historical unsupported centre microvia does not qualify the
remaining stack, current/return/thermal behavior or fabrication. Every original
threshold/current/protection/NTC/timer/default-off/part/HDI/process/fit/Gate A
hold remains. Zero opens is unfinished and fabrication remains prohibited.
