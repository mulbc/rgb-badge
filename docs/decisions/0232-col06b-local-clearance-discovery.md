# ADR 0232: COL_06_B local clearance discovery

Date: 2026-11-20
Status: Accepted for exact read-only cohort/contact and replacement discovery.

Use the immutable selected 25-open source. The local R6 gate transition in
ADR0231 is geometrically feasible, but source COL_06_B on In3 blocks its
separate proposed supply access at (75.250,4.250). The prior alternate lower
capture screen also has no complete candidate; its fixed gate centre at
(77.100,4.350) would conflict with assigning (76.650,4.550) to VLED. No
PCB or count reduction is inferred from any partial gate result.

Inventory the exact fifteen In3 COL_06_B staircase segments identified by
ADR0231's power-access blockers (all have width 0.20 mm). Prove they form a
single private path from (74.400,4.050) to (75.750,4.400), catalogue every
outside pad/Cu/filled-plane contact and retain all original source groups.
No source via, pad, layer or other color/row trace may be omitted.

Read-only screen replacement by three ordinary 0.20 mm In3 segments through
(74.400,Y) and (75.750,Y), with Y=2.850–3.800 mm in 0.050 mm steps.
Include all actual original outside endpoints, exact R6 B (75.750,3.750) /
0-degree pose, its previously clear complete gate at centre (77.100,4.350),
and its supported B/In4 plus In3/In4 supply accesses at (75.250,4.250) with
0.50 mm B lead. Omit prospectively only these fifteen exact color items and
the six R6 supply items frozen in ADR0119, retaining its shortened shared
In2 stem. Full source/mutual Cu and drill rules apply. This explicitly studies
using the In3 space freed by the earlier obsolete PAIR01 row-output tail;
that space is not presumed clear of other source nets.

A successful screen must still restore the R6 supply to an actual original
main-VLED port under the prior ≤8 mm pull-up bound, while keeping PAIR01.4
open, then freeze a complete prototype with exact retirement/contact/added
ledgers. Require every 213 original group and retained attached Cu intact,
matching lower counts, exact104, deterministic replay/refill, byte-exact
inverse/nine zone XORs, fault and visual gates before adoption. No current
limit, row-power qualification, symbol/part/footprint or copper width is changed.
Signal/return/thermal, trial package, fit/process/controlled-stack and independent
Gate A/fabrication holds remain open. Write no PCB in this discovery.
