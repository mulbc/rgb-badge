# ADR 0198: Supported full-stack sensor and selector prototype

Date: 2026-11-16
Status: Accepted for ignored complete-circuit feasibility only.

The ADR0197 three-layer graph exhausts without complete joins. Preserve its
exact fixed parts, 81-item selector cohort and supported prefixes/captures.
Combine only the independently verified exact seventeen-item GND bridge plane
substitution from ADR0181, for 98 original retirements total. Freeze the full
combined native contact catalog and require original outside GND plane
contacts to remain in the original main physical ground group. Keep every
other source copper item, part, rule, zone definition and safety limit fixed.

Permit bounded native ordinary routing across all six physical layers in
the same x=49–61 / y=6–21 mm window, 0.050 mm eight-neighbour grid and
750,000-node attempt limit. Only supported 0.30/0.10 mm adjacent outer
microvias; any new In2/In3 transition must be a mechanical buried 0.50/0.30
mm via. No unsupported centre microvia or full-span shortcut. Preserve the
full-board hole, original actual copper-edge/clearance and 0.20 mm ordinary
track rules. Full new supply ≤8 mm and selector ≤20 mm including prefixes.
At most 4 mm total new copper per net may occupy In2, in individual same-layer
segments ≤4 mm; this is feasibility, not return-path qualification.

After every join refill and retain every current group. Before selection,
restore all 216 original groups/interfaces, matching native/physical opens
below 28 and exact original 104 findings. Every source/part/pad/profile/type/
contact/hole/replay/inverse/orphan/fault/visual gate remains mandatory. Rail
splits, extra findings or incomplete restoration reject the candidate. All
electrical/thermal/current/protection/bypass/stack/process/fit/Gate A and
fabrication holds remain unchanged.
