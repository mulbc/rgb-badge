# ADR 0194: Fixed sensor outer escape discovery

Date: 2026-11-16
Status: Accepted for read-only complete-interface discovery only.

The ADR0193 bent necks fit ordinary B traces but no capture fits in that
narrow region. Retain all its source/cohort/rule constraints. Read-only
screen fixed U39 supply paths through (56.200,15.500), (56.200,16.250),
(56.300,16.250), (56.550,16.500) mm, then horizontally to x=57.000–58.500
and vertically to y=16.950–17.800 mm at 0.050 mm samples, total ordinary
0.20 mm B length ≤5 mm. The diagonal section must clear actual native pads
and retained copper; it creates no width exception. Screen supported
0.30/0.10 B/In4 captures, optional aligned In3/In4 access and actual mutual
clearance against ADR0189 selector prefixes. Also permit native eight-neighbour 0.050 mm B path discovery within
x=53–61 / y=13–20 mm and ≤8 mm total copper from the exact supply pad.
Every edge and proposed capture must clear native shapes and the actual board
outline; targets must be actual retained USB copper or supported captures.
This graph refinement may replace a blocked hand-defined prefix, without
changing its source cohort or width/hole rules. Do not omit either neighboring
colour or selector-six track, any other item or any part. Write no PCB.

Clearance discovery alone selects no successor. Both selector and full USB
supply continuity, all 216 original groups/interfaces, matching lower counts,
exact104 and every source/replay/inverse/contact/hole/orphan/fault/visual gate
remain required under separate exact complete-prototype scope. All electrical,
thermal, current/protection/bypass/stack/process/fit/Gate A/fabrication holds
remain unchanged.
