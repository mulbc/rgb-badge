# ADR 0195: Complete fixed sensor and selector prototype

Date: 2026-11-16
Status: Accepted for ignored complete-circuit feasibility only.

ADR0194 native B search finds sixteen mutually clear escapes. Freeze its
first cohort: selector supported aligned captures (52.350,7.850) mm with
the exact original-route B prefix from R30.1; fixed U39 ordinary B escape to
supported B/In4 capture (56.900,17.600) mm. Remove the redundant initial
within-pad grid reversal and collapse only same-direction collinear points,
then recheck every resulting native segment. Keep all parts and poses fixed.
Freeze the exact 80 original private selector items, signatures/types and full
native outside contacts before writing an ignored prepared board. A second exact alternative may use the last ADR0189 selector capture
(56.150,10.300) mm and its recorded original-route prefix, with ADR0194
power capture (57.950,17.600) mm and its recorded native B path. Recheck
mutual geometry and retain the same 20/8 mm complete-net limits. This
alternative changes no retirement or part pose. No other
original copper, via, pad, rule or zone definition may change.

Complete selector continuity to its original retained In3 interface and the
USB supply join to actual original same-net copper or individually witnessed
original supply-plane units. B/In4/In3 routing only, ordinary 0.20 mm tracks
and supported 0.30/0.10 mm adjacent outer microvias. Native 0.050 mm grid
with eight-neighbour exact edge checks, window x=49–61 / y=6–21 mm,
750,000 expanded nodes per attempt. Bound complete new selector copper to
20 mm and complete supply copper to 8 mm, including frozen prefixes. No
centre transition, smaller geometry or relaxed width/clearance/hole/edge rule.

Refill and preserve every current group after each join. Before selection
restore all 216 original groups and every layer-specific outside selector
contact; matching native/physical opens must fall below 28 with exact original
104 findings. Incomplete selector restoration, a rail split or new dangling
finding rejects the candidate. Full source/part/pad/profile/type/contact/hole/
replay/inverse/orphan/fault/visual gates remain mandatory. No electrical,
thermal, current/protection/bypass/stack/process/fit/Gate A/fabrication hold
changes; fixed C51/U40 locality remains unresolved.
