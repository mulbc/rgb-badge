# 1515 row and red escape continuation, 2026-10-16

Starting from the [1010 RGB trial](led-1010-rgb-hdi-screen-2026-10-16.md), a [generated all-row trial](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/led-all-row-1010-rgb-hdi-trial-114mm.kicad_pcb) now joins each 1010 front-layer anode bus to all eight checkerboard `QBLP1515A-RGB2A` anodes on In3.Cu. Its [generator](../../tools/generate-114mm-all-row-hdi-trial.py) extends each 1010 bus left to a stacked microvia at x = 40.6 mm, then joins eight 1515 anode lands through 0.20 mm inner tracks. It adds **432 provisional 0.30/0.10 mm microvias** and 144 segments. The 1515 anode vias sit 0.05 mm right of their pad centres, within their lands, to preserve clearance for red trunks. The full KiCad open-edge count falls from **1,022 to 894**; [native DRC](led-all-row-1010-rgb-hdi-drc-2026-10-16.json) still reports only the 104 intrinsic fine-pitch pad-gap findings. The deterministic board SHA-256 is `dc5ee0085c874fc31b87d8a6eaf24e335fab385b0f03ea8f6db1e6b2ddfd79b8`.

A further [red-column trial](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/led-all-row-1010-rgb-1515-red-hdi-trial-114mm.kicad_pcb) uses pad-centred F.Cu–In1.Cu microvias and 0.20 mm In1.Cu tracks for all eight 1515 red columns. Its [generator](../../tools/generate-114mm-1515-red-hdi-trial.py) adds 128 microvias and 120 segments. Full open edges fall to **774**, and [native DRC](led-all-row-1010-rgb-1515-red-hdi-drc-2026-10-16.json) still has only the 104 pre-existing findings. Its deterministic SHA-256 is `f52a58f65258c73e71cf876a95c43c65c1c6b5ca54316b680744f61a33e9b96a`.

Two rejected checks guide the next routing step. Direct front-copper lines between 1515 anodes produced **112 shorts** against cathode lands. Centered blue F.Cu–In1.Cu/In1.Cu–In2.Cu stacks and straight blue In2.Cu trunks added **92 shorts**, because several blue via lands intersect the sloping red tracks on In1.Cu. The blue and green paths therefore need coordinated red-track detours or a different layer/via pattern; they cannot copy the red path mechanically.

Both boards are **unselected HDI geometry studies**. Via-in-pad filling/capping, solder process, 1.0 mm six-layer stack-up, yield, current capacity and supplier capability are unresolved. The colour columns and anode rows still do not reach their drivers. Blue/green on the 1515 half, power rerouting, RF/case/pack fit and Gate A review remain. Native DRC does not approve fabrication.
