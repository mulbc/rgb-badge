# Bounded follow-on screens from the accepted 59-edge board

Date: 2026-10-30. Coupon Rev A is unbuilt; these are first-author source/geometry experiments, not hardware measurements. Firmware is unchanged. Accepted source is commit `d88599a`, board SHA-256 `a28258edac032d3dd047f2d7404c0752b25c472af4236b4618b3854717ddfc62`, with **59 opens / 104 unchanged intrinsic findings**. No new connection or package is selected by this record.

An ignored experimental eight-direction native-grid search makes 16 endpoint attempts across 15 digital nets at 0.05 mm, retaining original 0.20 mm clearance and the ADR 0047 120 mm per-net bound. Thirteen attempts exhaust their reachable grid, two hit the 700,000-node bound, and USB_EN1_RAW_N produces an endpoint join that splits old ground/application-supply groups. No attempted route passes the full group gate. The [raw search report](led-routing-progress-59-follow-on-search-2026-10-30.json) records inputs, helper hashes, outcomes and the rejected board fingerprint. The private experimental helper remains ignored; it is not a new published or CI-qualified router. Finite grid results establish no general impossibility.

The rejected USB trial has 60 native opens and the same 104 intrinsic findings. One 0.20 mm In4 ground bridge with two adjacent In4/B microvias, from (41.35, 12.845) to (41.35, 11.845) mm, restores its four-pad ground group and returns the count to 59. Its old application-supply group remains split. Removing each of nine new In4 USB track components in turn also fails to restore that entire supply group. No such candidate is adopted: a new digital join cannot compensate for disconnecting a previous supply group. Trial copper and its local native reports stay in the ignored build tree.

A separate native pad/portal screen identifies the exact original COL_05_B In4 track (84.75, 8.90) → (77.95, 8.90) mm beneath otherwise clear SCLK/LAT escape sites. [ADR 0068](../decisions/0068-driver-clock-latch-colour-transfer-feasibility.md) bounds a full-width local transfer to original plus 2 mm, ±1 mm vertical displacement, and adjacent vias. All **7,752 enumerated In3/B doglegs** fail complete other-net geometry before refill. In3 via sites encounter existing COL_12_G and MODE_BOOT_N copper; B sites encounter SCLK/LAT/SIN pads and retained LED_SIN/ROW_SEL_03 copper. A future coordinated rework needs its own exact interfaces, retention and full acceptance evidence; these results authorize no wider deletion or smaller spacing.

[Machine-readable results](../../mechanical/review/routing-progress-59-follow-on-2026-10-30.json) pin the accepted source, copied raw search output and local raw-log hashes/locations. The accepted board, plan, catalogs, packages, electrical settings and all 59-edge native/replay/fault evidence remain unchanged. The 50-edge target, Murata shared-land migration, engineering qualification and independent Gate A remain open.
