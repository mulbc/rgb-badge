# Ordered sixteen-row pair banks on the 114 mm matrix trial

The prior onsemi [first-row study](led-onsemi-row-matrix-study-2026-10-16.md) had candidate pairs scattered from x ≈ 12 to 113 mm. A new [deterministic first-fit screen](../../mechanical/review/row-bank-114mm-screen.json) reserves two ordered rear-side banks at x = 74.0–77.5 and 78.0–81.5 mm. It reuses the 114 × 32.5 mm outline and preserves the fixed battery-facing TH1/U39 reservation. Because the selected front-layer 1010 row topology needs no row-anode via sites, this screen does not reserve the 103 unused via keepouts. It places all **227 counted rear-side entities** in one greedy order, including sixteen 3.5 × 3.5 mm pair boxes. This is an XY screen, not routing or enclosure fit.

The [generated native board](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/led-qt-uniform-2p2-row-bank-onsemi-trial-114mm.kicad_pcb) starts from the accepted coupon-pitch complete-matrix trial, moves the non-LED rear parts to the screened boxes, and substitutes the exact numbered candidate onsemi `NTLJD3119CTBG` footprints for sixteen netless Diodes review boxes. Its [generator](../../tools/generate-114mm-row-bank-matrix-trial.py) then joins **all sixteen `PAIRxx` pin-3 anode outputs to their complete LED rows**: one short B.Cu breakout, one 0.50/0.30 mm through-via and one In3.Cu link per row. The other row-pair pins have assigned candidate nets but no routes.

Six complete colour paths now reach actual TLC59581 U1 pins. Four 1515 paths use staggered upper-edge transitions on In4.Cu: `COL_08_R`→36, `COL_13_R`→53, `COL_14_R`→2 and `COL_15_B`→7. Two 1010 paths leave from the lower edge: `COL_00_R`→8 on In4.Cu, and `COL_07_R`→33 on In2.Cu with a short detour around the row-15 stacked via. Each driver land uses one or more candidate B-side microvias. These are **six fanout feasibility checks**, not a 48-channel escape. Full KiCad connectivity has **640 open edges**, 22 fewer than the placed-and-netted board before these joins. Native [KiCad 10.0.6 DRC](led-qt-uniform-2p2-row-bank-onsemi-drc-2026-10-16.json) reports only the **104 intrinsic fine-pitch pad-gap findings**, with no new geometric finding. The board and screen regenerated twice with SHA-256 `898d9f5f4c802512abe8739a75334250d69ff3e7a138343b87320de5be405df2` and `e0aeb6740063cf4fa0abe71047b1ac9b2ca0251995880ef018e506bc169ce4e5`, respectively.

This remains a **mixed-source placement/routing study**. The uniform 2.2 mm QT coupon geometry is accepted under [ADR 0034](../decisions/0034-uniform-qt-coupon-pitch-proposal.md), but the candidate onsemi row circuit/BOM are not selected or synchronized with the canonical schematic. The left bank lies partly under the projected pack; rear-side component height, pouch isolation, swelling space and thermal paths need CAD and physical qualification. The other 42 colour-column driver joins, row select/gate/VLED/GND routes, USB/charger/power/control copper, RF, case fit, HDI process, onsemi stencil/heat and independent Gate A remain open. No board has been built or measured.
