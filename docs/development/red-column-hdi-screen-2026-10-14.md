# Red-column HDI geometry screen, 2026-10-14

The [generated KiCad study](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/red-column-hdi-trial-120mm.kicad_pcb) copies the current **120 × 32.5 mm**, provisional six-layer, 1.0 mm coupon placement. It is an **unselected routing experiment**, not the canonical PCB, a stack-up decision, an assembler-approved via-in-pad design, or a fabrication release. The [generator](../../tools/generate-red-column-hdi-trial.py) leaves every existing footprint and trace in place, then connects the red cathodes in all sixteen 16-pixel columns. Columns 0–7 use Everlight pad 2; columns 8–15 use QT Brightek pad 4, including their captured checkerboard rotations. Every red cathode receives a tentative **0.3/0.1 mm F.Cu–In1.Cu microvia at the LED pad centre**; successive red pads join with 0.20 mm In1.Cu segments. This adds **256 microvias and 240 segments**. No driver output reaches these columns yet.

The file regenerated twice with identical SHA-256 `061ed9aca073db0422321570440a7c73526661f5db978d4a088a91bae1258fe2`. KiCad reload found all 497 original footprints; the study has 445 segments and 273 total vias. Native KiCad 10.0.6 DRC reported the same **eight pre-existing U48/U52 intra-pad 0.18 mm clearance findings** and no new geometric violations (`/private/tmp/rgb-red-column-staged-drc.json`). Its 499 unconnected-item listing is capped. The full native connectivity count decreased from **1,453 to 1,213 open edges**, exactly 15 per red column. This validates only the saved net/geometry topology at the current design rules. It does not prove electrical current capacity, mask/paste quality, solder fill, yield, stack-up availability or cost.

The earlier direct front-copper probe shorted row anodes, and its pad-avoiding detour crossed a front row trunk. The inner-layer red pattern avoids those crossings, but **green and blue remain entirely unrouted**. Each LED style places at least two cathode pads at the same X coordinate on different Y positions, so copying all three colour trunks onto In1 would create further same-layer conflicts. A complete pattern needs verified colour breakout, driver fanout, row-switch joins, ground/power return, and a manufacturable via and copper stack. Via-in-pad microvias may require filling/capping and a compatible 1.0 mm six-layer process; no supplier has approved that process. The 120 mm study also exceeds the 110 mm finished-envelope target. Keep the main 120 mm placement study unchanged while resolving these conditions and the independent Gate A review.

A temporary copy tested that colour-sharing question by adding all sixteen blue columns with identical F.Cu–In1.Cu pad-centred microvias and direct inner segments. Native DRC then found **194 geometric violations**: the original eight clearances plus 112 shorts and 74 track crossings, mainly where the QT checkerboard makes red/blue paths exchange lateral order. The raw report is `/private/tmp/rgb-red-blue-in1-drc.json`; the temporary board was removed. Therefore a second colour cannot simply copy the red straight-run pattern onto In1. This does not prove that another same-layer detour or an alternative layer scheme is impossible.

Regenerate with KiCad's bundled Python: `/Applications/KiCad/KiCad.app/Contents/Frameworks/Python.framework/Versions/3.9/bin/python3 tools/generate-red-column-hdi-trial.py`. Run native `kicad-cli pcb drc --format json` on the generated board in the project directory, and use `tools/report-coupon-board-connectivity.py` for the uncapped edge count. The DRC findings are source checks, not bench measurements; no physical board or firmware revision was tested.
