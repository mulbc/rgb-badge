# R6 local gate and supply screen

No successor is selected. The corrected source remains byte-identical at
35 native/physical opens. [ADR 0119](../decisions/0119-r6-local-gate-and-vled-capture-study.md)
permits a bounded R6 relocation and exact six-item supply-leaf replacement,
but the preliminary geometry screen does not produce a complete candidate.
No PCB is saved, no source copper is retired and no new via is adopted.

The reproducible native screen tests 62,468 finite poses over x=71–79 mm,
y=0.8–5.6 mm, on a 0.05 mm grid at four right-angle orientations. Of those,
48,286 fail fixed courtyards and 9,262 fail native pad/copper or edge screening.
All 4,920 remaining poses fail a coincident four-transition supply capture.
Testing without any capture also finds no permitted direct or single-corner
B.Cu gate lead within 3 mm. This does not exclude a different multi-corner
route, staggered capture, unsampled pose or coordinated placement.

An additional 10,556-point outer-capture screen finds 7,238 points blocked
on B.Cu and the remaining 3,318 on In4.Cu. In particular, the retained
ROW_GATE_05 In4 track at y=4.25 mm blocks capture at the R11/PAIR01 VLED
lands; source ROW_SEL_01/03 B.Cu routing blocks representative gate approaches.
A direct transfer of that gate track to In3 is itself obstructed by original
row-current and colour routing. These are identified obstacles, not permission
to remove those connections or relax widths/clearance.

The [machine-readable record](../../mechanical/review/r6-locality-2026-11-12.json)
contains exact source/tool hashes, counts, fixed-item witnesses and ignored
raw evidence locations. No new native connectivity, refill or full-DRC result
is inferred from this screen. More coordinated placement/routing needs its
own exact contact and replacement ledger. All electrical/process/fit/stack
qualification and independent Gate A holds remain; fabrication is prohibited.
