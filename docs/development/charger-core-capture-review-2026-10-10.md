# Rev A provisional BQ24074 charger-core capture, 2026-10-10

The root-linked `charger-core.kicad_sch` places exact `BQ24074RGTR`, `ERA2AEB2491X` on ISET, `ERA2AEB3651X` on ILIM, one Murata 1 µF/10 V IN capacitor and two Murata 10 µF/6.3 V BAT/OUT capacitors. The charger TS pin joins the already root-linked battery-facing `NCU15XH103F60RC` NTC. BQ OUT replaces the application SYS source flag. Both BQ grounds and exposed pad join GND. BQ OUT pin 11 is encoded as passive in the KiCad symbol solely to avoid ERC treating the two internally common OUT pads as independent fighting sources; pin 10 remains a power output. This is an ERC modelling choice, not a changed manufacturer pin assignment.

`CE` and `EN1` are held low. `EN2` is also held low until the charger input gate and qualified mode drive are captured, so the present fallback mode is USB100 (100 mA input ceiling) and the installed ILIM resistor is inactive. Open `ITERM` uses TI's default 10%-of-fast-charge termination. Open `TMR` uses TI's active default 30-minute nominal precharge and five-hour nominal fast-charge timers; it is not grounded or disabled. `PGOOD` and `CHG` are explicitly unconnected for now. Exact battery/header polarity and E1 gate are not represented. The `CHARGER_IN_DRAFT` and `+BAT_PROTECTED_DRAFT` PWR_FLAGs exist solely to let KiCad check the provisional boundary and are not physical sources.

First-author source/native evidence: KiCad CLI 10.0.6, 2026-10-10, generated under `/private/tmp/rgb-charger-full4-20261010/`. ERC returned zero configured violations. Native XML contained 426 PCB items and 1,589 logical pins; source and XML checkers verified all 27 new pin nets, including TH1 on TS. The sixteen-page PDF was rendered and page 16 inspected for visible pin labels, NC markers, ground and supply boundaries. No board, firmware, external supply or cell was connected; these are source checks, not bench measurements.

The complete host regression suite passed 168 tests. A first run exposed one stale message assertion after the captured charger changed the current source-state wording; the wording was corrected and the entire suite rerun successfully. `git diff --check` reported no whitespace errors.

Remaining before complete charger capture: E0/E1 source protection and permission with default-off EN behavior, EN2 external-mode drive, delivered-pack PHR-2 polarity/header wiring, capacitor DC-bias and fault-voltage suitability, BQ-to-pack thermal coupling and pack timing under low ILIM, charge-status disposition, then independent Gate A review. The lower ILIM and built-in timer are design selections for this draft, not evidence of safe charger behavior. Manufacturer evidence: [TI BQ24074 Rev N](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq24074.pdf), [Panasonic ERA2AEB2491X](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/high-precision-chip-resistors/models/ERA2AEB2491X), and the project-local part audits.
