# Full-footprint 120 mm coupon placement trial, 2026-10-13

This is a **staged, unrouted electrical-layout study**, not the canonical coupon PCB, case fit, or fabrication source. It intentionally extends the trial board to **120 × 32.5 mm**, beyond the finished badge's **110 × 35 × 11 mm** maximum case. The wider study uses all **32 currently connected row transistors**; it does not substitute the unverified `DMC1229UFDB-7` package. A new 120 mm first-fit pass could assign all rear-screened courtyards space, while the same algorithm at 110 mm left 13 canonical parts unplaced without bypass grouping. This is a heuristic result and does not prove 120 mm routing or establish a minimum board length.

`tools/generate-full-coupon-placement-trial.py` exports the current root schematic's native KiCad XML, places all **497 exact project-local footprints** with **1,814 unique numbered pin-to-net assignments**, and saves a separate KiCad PCB source at [staging/full-coupon-placement-trial-120mm.kicad_pcb](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/full-coupon-placement-trial-120mm.kicad_pcb). The front has the 16 × 16 Everlight/QT comparison grid at 1.95 mm pitch, with the QT 0°/90° checkerboard. The rear uses current first-fit courtyard boxes; J1 is on the right and U1/U3 use existing study positions. No tracks, vias, zones, USB mid-mount cutout, mounting holes, battery insulation, case walls or local converter routing are present. The board source is reproducible; its current SHA-256 is `071969fdf1c19c6179c67d2b92147ca8bb7da242cedd2460f782a34cdf1a22a0`.

Initial native KiCad 10.0.6 PCB DRC reported 12 geometric violations and 499 unconnected items. Two were copper-to-edge misses at Q1 and TP4; their study positions were moved 0.15 mm inward. Two were silkscreen markers on the SW1 and U3 pads; their project-local footprint markers were moved clear of copper without changing any pad, courtyard or package geometry. `tools/check-kicad.sh` passed native library exports, source connectivity and zero schematic ERC after those library changes. The regenerated board's native DRC now reports **eight geometric violations**, all the already-known 0.18 mm copper gap within U48/U52 versus the 0.20 mm default clearance, and **499 unconnected items**. There are no inter-footprint copper-clearance violations in this unrouted placement. The opens are expected because the trial has no tracks; **PCB DRC does not pass**. No schematic-to-PCB parity check, USB mechanical cutout check or manufacturing-process approval was run. Native front/back SVG exports were inspected for gross placement and face assignment only.

This study gives the next routing attempt a complete set of actual pads and net names, but its area-first packing scatters related circuitry and ignores bypass, power-loop, RF and test-access needs. The next useful PCB step is to group and route the protected USB/charger, both converters and the interlock locally on this source-derived study, then revisit board width. If the final target cannot fit with canonical row parts, the paired-row package needs a verified numeric pin map and 0.15 mm fabrication-process decision before adoption. The independent Gate A review remains required before any fabrication.

Reproduce with KiCad's bundled Python: `/Applications/KiCad/KiCad.app/Contents/Frameworks/Python.framework/Versions/3.9/bin/python3 tools/generate-full-coupon-placement-trial.py`. Then run `kicad-cli pcb drc --format json --output <report.json> hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/full-coupon-placement-trial-120mm.kicad_pcb` in the native desktop environment; inspect `.violations` and `.unconnected_items` separately. The raw report for this run was `/private/tmp/rgb-full-placement-120mm-drc.json`.
