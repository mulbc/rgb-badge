# Full-footprint 120 mm coupon placement trial, 2026-10-13

This is a **staged, minimally routed electrical-layout study**, not the canonical coupon PCB, case fit, or fabrication source. It intentionally extends the trial board to **120 × 32.5 mm**, beyond the finished badge's **110 × 35 × 11 mm** maximum case. The wider study uses all **32 currently connected row transistors**; it does not substitute the unverified `DMC1229UFDB-7` package. A new 120 mm first-fit pass could assign all rear-screened courtyards space, while the same algorithm at 110 mm left 13 canonical parts unplaced without bypass grouping. This is a heuristic result and does not prove 120 mm routing or establish a minimum board length.

`tools/generate-full-coupon-placement-trial.py` exports the current root schematic's native KiCad XML, places all **497 exact project-local footprints** with **1,814 unique numbered pin-to-net assignments**, and saves a separate KiCad PCB source at [staging/full-coupon-placement-trial-120mm.kicad_pcb](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/full-coupon-placement-trial-120mm.kicad_pcb). The board now has the plan's provisional **six copper layers and 1.0 mm thickness**, instead of KiCad's two-layer/1.6 mm defaults. This is not a qualified stack-up: the USB4505 guide calls for 0.80 mm, and connector/stack compatibility remains to be resolved. The front has the 16 × 16 Everlight/QT comparison grid at 1.95 mm pitch, with the QT 0°/90° checkerboard. The rear uses current first-fit courtyard boxes except for a small USB input cluster: E0 U45 and E1 U44 are now beside J1, with C60/C61/C62, C52/C57 and their five E0/E1 programming resistors nearby. The VLED converter IC U37 and inductor L2 occupy their prior local-study boxes, while the rest of that group remains first-fit. J1 is on the right and U1/U3 use existing study positions. The generator reloads the saved board and checks all footprint IDs and numbered pad nets against the exported schematic. Four local E0/E1 dVdt and ILM support links are routed on rear copper with eight 0.20 mm segments. Both J1 VBUS pads reach C60 and E0 input through three 0.6/0.3 mm through vias and nine 0.20 mm front/inner/rear segments. E0 output to C61 uses two more through vias and four rear/inner segments. Two U37-to-L2 switching nets use six rear-copper segments, including both paired IC pads on each net. The eight Everlight anode pads in each of 16 rows now join a 0.20 mm front-copper trial trunk, adding 144 segments. The QT half-row anodes, row switches, LED cathodes and essentially all other connections remain open; no zones, USB mid-mount cutout, mounting holes, battery insulation or case walls are present. The converter has no completed input/output power loop. The board source is reproducible; its current SHA-256 is `ceadf1c366ca947cc13dde915b3c13de5a29f2960b315547d15b452fb8dc9917`.

Initial native KiCad 10.0.6 PCB DRC reported 12 geometric violations and 499 unconnected items. Two were copper-to-edge misses at Q1 and TP4; their study positions were moved 0.15 mm inward. Two were silkscreen markers on the SW1 and U3 pads; their project-local footprint markers were moved clear of copper without changing any pad, courtyard or package geometry. `tools/check-kicad.sh` passed native library exports, source connectivity and zero schematic ERC after those library changes. The regenerated six-layer board with four support links and 16 Everlight half-row trunks still reports **eight geometric violations**, all the already-known 0.18 mm copper gap within U48/U52 versus the 0.20 mm default clearance, and **499 reported unconnected items**. The 499 figure appeared before and after both routing increments, so it must not be read as a complete or decreasing open-net count; the board remains overwhelmingly unrouted and **PCB DRC does not pass**. There are no new track-clearance violations in the project-context DRC run. The 0.20 mm row trunks are geometry trials, not reviewed current-capacity or thermal paths. The round-trip footprint/pad-net check passed. No USB mechanical cutout check or manufacturing-process approval was run. Native front/back SVG exports were inspected for gross placement and face assignment only.

The native `GetUnconnectedCount(False)` connectivity API gives an uncapped progress measure: **1,590 open connection edges** on the unrouted `f6adcbd` board, **1,586** after the four support links at `8d9d0ab`, **1,474** after the Everlight trunks, and **1,466** on this 171-segment/five-via board. The 16 eight-pad Everlight trunks account for 112 removed edges (seven per row), and the four converter links remove another four, the E0 output capacitor path removes one, and the J1-to-E0 input/C60/second connector-pad links remove three. The actual remaining work is much larger than the CLI DRC's repeated 499-item listing. Reproduce with KiCad's bundled Python and `tools/report-coupon-board-connectivity.py`. These are KiCad ratsnest edges, not independent circuit nets or a measure of safety.

The input cluster improves the J1-to-E0 proximity and makes the E0/E1 support-pad geometry available for routing review. The larger E1 output capacitor C56 still sits remotely: reserving its local box displaced U35 in this first-fit pass, so that option was rejected. The area-first remainder still scatters related circuitry and ignores many bypass, power-loop, RF and test-access needs. The next useful PCB step is to complete the protected USB/charger local placement and routing, then both converters and the interlock, and revisit board width. If the final target cannot fit with canonical row parts, the paired-row package needs a verified numeric pin map and 0.15 mm fabrication-process decision before adoption. The independent Gate A review remains required before any fabrication.

**VLED core trial:** U37 is rotated to put its switching pads beside L2; L2 is rotated so its correct two pad nets face those pads. Six 0.20 mm rear-copper segments connect both U37 switching-pad pairs (8/9 and 6/7) to L2 pads 1/2. This is a topology and clearance probe, not a current-rated converter layout. Native KiCad DRC after this change still has the same eight known 0.18 mm U48/U52 pad gaps and 499 capped open items; no new geometric violation was reported. A fixed 13-part VLED cluster displaced Q30–Q32 and U35 in the 120 mm first-fit pass. Fixing either C44 or C46 in the local cluster also displaced U50; fixing both displaced U49 and U50. Those alternatives were rejected so the study retains all 497 schematic parts. The converter's input/output capacitors and feedback loop still need a compact placement and complete routing before any electrical conclusion.

**USB output escape screen:** A temporary rear-copper route from U45 pad 6 to C61 pad 1 shorted U45's adjacent OVLO pad when drawn directly. A bent escape avoided that pad but shorted U45's unconnected pad 4 and bridged its mask aperture. Standard 0.6/0.3 mm through vias at (105.5, 17.0) and (110.55, 16.8) mm, joined on In4.Cu, instead connect those two pads with no new native DRC geometric findings. The positive-side path is in the staged board; it is a geometry trial, not a current-rated power route. Its ground return and E1/charger links remain open. Both J1 VBUS pads are joined to the E0 input net, while USB ground and data/CC routes remain open. Raw temporary DRC reports: `/private/tmp/rgb-usb-e0-cap-drc.json`, `/private/tmp/rgb-usb-e0-cap-drc2.json` and `/private/tmp/rgb-e0-via-probe-drc3.json`; generated-board DRC: `/private/tmp/rgb-j1-vbus-both-staged-drc.json`. The input path uses a second 0.6/0.3 mm via pair at (106.0, 12.5) and (110.2, 13.2) mm, with the latter also feeding J1 pad B4/A9 on F.Cu. A fifth via at (112.2, 18.65) mm joins J1 pad A4/B9 on In4.Cu. These remain geometry trials without current-capacity qualification.

**Via escape screen, unselected:** On a temporary copy of this exact board, 0.6/0.3 mm through-via escapes from the 16 Everlight row trunks found front/rear pad-clear sites, but KiCad flagged 16 dangling vias because no inner routes to the row switches were added. A separate three-QT-pixel sample (D9–D11) using the same through-via size produced new front clearances and rear shorts at C49/C53. Three 0.3/0.1 mm F.Cu–In1.Cu microvias plus two short inner segments cleared those three sample pads without new copper-geometry findings. The temporary-copy DRC reports are `/private/tmp/rgb-test-row-vias-drc.json`, `/private/tmp/rgb-test-qt-vias-drc.json` and `/private/tmp/rgb-test-qt-microvias-drc.json`; each includes 199 unrelated missing-library warnings because the temporary file was outside the project's library-table directory. This is a **three-pixel geometry comparison**, not proof that the QT half or full matrix can be routed; no microvia or HDI process has been selected. A fixed-location attempt to bring C56 beside E1 displaced U35, then L2 and L1 as their old sites were reserved; it was reverted rather than silently dropping parts.
