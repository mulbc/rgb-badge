# Full-footprint 120 mm coupon placement trial, 2026-10-13

This is a **staged, minimally routed electrical-layout study**, not the canonical coupon PCB, case fit, or fabrication source. It intentionally extends the trial board to **120 × 32.5 mm**, beyond the finished badge's **110 × 35 × 11 mm** maximum case. The wider study uses all **32 currently connected row transistors**; it does not substitute the unverified `DMC1229UFDB-7` package. A new 120 mm first-fit pass could assign all rear-screened courtyards space, while the same algorithm at 110 mm left 13 canonical parts unplaced without bypass grouping. This is a heuristic result and does not prove 120 mm routing or establish a minimum board length.

`tools/generate-full-coupon-placement-trial.py` exports the current root schematic's native KiCad XML, places all **497 exact project-local footprints** with **1,814 unique numbered pin-to-net assignments**, and saves a separate KiCad PCB source at [staging/full-coupon-placement-trial-120mm.kicad_pcb](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/full-coupon-placement-trial-120mm.kicad_pcb). The board now has the plan's provisional **six copper layers and 1.0 mm thickness**, instead of KiCad's two-layer/1.6 mm defaults. This is not a qualified stack-up: the USB4505 guide calls for 0.80 mm, and connector/stack compatibility remains to be resolved. The front has the 16 × 16 Everlight/QT comparison grid at 1.95 mm pitch, with the QT 0°/90° checkerboard. The rear uses current first-fit courtyard boxes except for a small USB input cluster: E0 U45 and E1 U44 are now beside J1, with C60/C61/C62, C52/C57 and their five E0/E1 programming resistors nearby. The VLED converter IC U37 and inductor L2 occupy their prior local-study boxes, while the rest of that group remains first-fit. U41 and C56 are now grouped below the USB connector, and U41 is rotated to put its IN pad near C56. J1 is on the right and U1/U3 use existing study positions. The generator reloads the saved board and checks all footprint IDs and numbered pad nets against the exported schematic. Four local E0/E1 dVdt and ILM support links are routed on rear copper with eight 0.20 mm segments. Both J1 VBUS pads reach C60 and E0 input through three 0.6/0.3 mm through vias and nine 0.20 mm front/inner/rear segments. E0 output to C61 uses two more through vias and four rear/inner segments. E1 input joins that protected rail with two rear segments, and E1 output reaches C52 using two further vias and five rear/inner segments. C52 then reaches U41 IN and C56 through one more via and four inner/rear segments. Two U37-to-L2 switching nets use six rear-copper segments, including both paired IC pads on each net. The eight Everlight anode pads in each of 16 rows now join a 0.20 mm front-copper trial trunk, adding 144 segments. The QT half-row anodes, row switches, LED cathodes and essentially all other connections remain open; no zones, USB mid-mount cutout, mounting holes, battery insulation or case walls are present. The converter has no completed input/output power loop. The board source is reproducible; its current SHA-256 is `a22901c1730562ce623257e74f64279335cc2baea8e5b1183293b616559fdfb6`.

Initial native KiCad 10.0.6 PCB DRC reported 12 geometric violations and 499 unconnected items. Two were copper-to-edge misses at Q1 and TP4; their study positions were moved 0.15 mm inward. Two were silkscreen markers on the SW1 and U3 pads; their project-local footprint markers were moved clear of copper without changing any pad, courtyard or package geometry. `tools/check-kicad.sh` passed native library exports, source connectivity and zero schematic ERC after those library changes. The regenerated six-layer board with the current partial USB/VLED and Everlight routing still reports **eight geometric violations**, all the already-known 0.18 mm copper gap within U48/U52 versus the 0.20 mm default clearance, and **499 reported unconnected items**. The 499 figure persisted across routing increments, so it must not be read as a complete or decreasing open-net count; the board remains overwhelmingly unrouted and **PCB DRC does not pass**. There are no new track-clearance violations in the project-context DRC run. The 0.20 mm row trunks are geometry trials, not reviewed current-capacity or thermal paths. The round-trip footprint/pad-net check passed. No USB mechanical cutout check or manufacturing-process approval was run. Native front/back SVG exports were inspected for gross placement and face assignment only.

The native `GetUnconnectedCount(False)` connectivity API gives an uncapped progress measure: **1,590 open connection edges** on the unrouted `f6adcbd` board, **1,586** after the four support links at `8d9d0ab`, **1,474** after the Everlight trunks, and **1,462** on this 182-segment/eight-via board. The 16 eight-pad Everlight trunks account for 112 removed edges (seven per row), and the four converter links remove another four, the E0 output capacitor path removes one, the J1-to-E0 input/C60/second connector-pad links remove three, the E1 input/output joins remove two, and the U41/C56 charger-input joins remove two. The actual remaining work is much larger than the CLI DRC's repeated 499-item listing. Reproduce with KiCad's bundled Python and `tools/report-coupon-board-connectivity.py`. These are KiCad ratsnest edges, not independent circuit nets or a measure of safety.

The input cluster improves the J1-to-E0 proximity and makes the E0/E1 support-pad geometry available for routing review. The first attempt to fix C56 near E1 displaced U35; regrouping U41 and C56 below J1 instead retains every part and brings C56 positive within about 2.1 mm XY of the rotated U41 IN pad. This is only courtyard and copper geometry; the USB mid-mount cutout, connector body, assembly access, height and charger return path still need review. The area-first remainder still scatters related circuitry and ignores many bypass, power-loop, RF and test-access needs. The next useful PCB step is to complete USB/charger ground returns, power-copper widths and local support placement, then both converters and the interlock, and revisit board width. If the final target cannot fit with canonical row parts, the paired-row package needs a verified numeric pin map and 0.15 mm fabrication-process decision before adoption. The independent Gate A review remains required before any fabrication.

**VLED core trial:** U37 is rotated to put its switching pads beside L2; L2 is rotated so its correct two pad nets face those pads. Six 0.20 mm rear-copper segments connect both U37 switching-pad pairs (8/9 and 6/7) to L2 pads 1/2. This is a topology and clearance probe, not a current-rated converter layout. Native KiCad DRC after this change still has the same eight known 0.18 mm U48/U52 pad gaps and 499 capped open items; no new geometric violation was reported. A fixed 13-part VLED cluster displaced Q30–Q32 and U35 in the 120 mm first-fit pass. Fixing either C44 or C46 in the local cluster also displaced U50; fixing both displaced U49 and U50. Those alternatives were rejected so the study retains all 497 schematic parts. The converter's input/output capacitors and feedback loop still need a compact placement and complete routing before any electrical conclusion.
