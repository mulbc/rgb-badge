# 114 mm placement with provisional 1010 LED via reservations

The [deterministic screen](../../tools/screen-114mm-led-via-keepouts.py) starts from the 114 × 32.5 mm paired-row placement study, retains the fixed battery-facing TH1/U39 and the local U29 C30/C32 capacitor slots, and reserves 0.9 × 0.9 mm rear-side keepouts around candidate 0.50/0.30 mm through-via centers for the 1010 LED row-anode escapes. Of 105 candidate sites across seven interior columns and 15 rows, 103 do not overlap fixed boxes. The other two intersect TH1 and still require a different escape or sensor/placement change. A first-fit run with all 103 reservations places every counted component box at 114 mm; its [machine-readable result](../../mechanical/review/led-via-aware-114mm-screen.json) is a conditional XY result, not a complete route.

The separate [native placement board](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/led-via-aware-placement-trial-114mm.kicad_pcb) contains 483 real footprints, no traces and no vias; full KiCad connectivity has 1,494 open edges. KiCad CLI 10.0.6 reports the same **104 intrinsic fine-pitch pad-gap findings** as the earlier unrouted study, with no added placement finding; the [raw DRC report](led-via-aware-placement-drc-2026-10-15.json) truncates unconnected items at 499. The generated board's SHA-256 is `59b7659096e49ed351ae36618fc567a160b5ab93e1a2ecaa44bfec6f941d7528`.

For an initial geometry probe, 103 actual 0.50/0.30 mm vias and 0.20 mm front-layer two-segment stubs were added at the reserved sites. Native DRC reported **104 baseline clearances plus 103 expected dangling-via findings**, with no new clearance, hole or mask violation. That temporary board was discarded.

The retained [1010 row-escape trial](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/led-via-aware-row-escape-trial-114mm.kicad_pcb) connects the reserved vias within each of rows 00–14 on In1.Cu. Two short front-layer links bring the anodes blocked by TH1 to their row's neighboring via. Row 15 instead joins all eight 1010 anodes on a front-layer bottom-edge bus with one offset via; seven repeated inter-LED vias at the bottom edge failed the 0.50 mm copper-to-edge rule. The generated trial has **244 segments, 104 vias and 1,397 full KiCad open edges**, down from 1,494 on its unrouted placement parent. Native DRC has the same **104 intrinsic pad-gap findings plus one expected dangling-via finding** at the row-15 offset via; there are no added clearance, hole, mask or edge violations. Its [raw DRC report](led-via-aware-row-escape-drc-2026-10-15.json) is retained, and the board SHA-256 is `9b2c7223f39ae90ab2c68deaeaf19c67e8d4a130be4aabff80905cdbf72576b3`.

A second [1010 front-row alternative](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/led-1010-front-row-bus-trial-114mm.kicad_pcb) places a 0.20 mm front-layer bus and eight pad stubs on **each of all 16 rows**. It needs **144 segments and zero vias**, reduces full KiCad open edges to **1,382**, and has only the same [104 baseline DRC findings](led-1010-front-row-bus-drc-2026-10-15.json). Its deterministic SHA-256 is `107e6bf74c6ddfa84c93f0fdb0ee308175eb70903c0cb2d2d28ae98e6852e65e`. This is a better row-only geometry, but it occupies the inter-row channels where the via-escape option would place vias. Neither row option solves the three colour-column nets per pixel; they are alternatives, not copper to combine blindly.

The power-routed study is a different board. Moving rear-side parts to reserve LED vias invalidates its tentative traces; no power copper was copied to either row variant. The 1010 rows still need connection to their row drivers and the 1515 anodes; the 1515 LED escapes, all colour-column routes, power-trunk rerouting, current/return layout, enclosure/RF fit and independent Gate A review remain. The 0.50/0.30 mm via process is provisional. Neither this study nor the source-code checks approve fabrication.
