# Battery-facing charger-temperature capture review, 2026-10-10

Scope: first-author review of the `coupon-power-rev-a` KiCad source after root-linking `charger-temperature.kicad_sch`. This records schematic connectivity and drawing checks, not a bench measurement, independent Gate A review, or battery qualification. No board has been built.

The sheet adds ten PCB items and 25 logical pins: Murata `NCU15XH103F60RC` NTC, TI `TMP390A2DRLR` and `SN74AUP1G125DBVR`, Panasonic `ERJ2RKF2151X`, `ERJ2RKF1402X` and `ERJ2RKF1002X`, two Murata `GRM155R71C104KA88D` bypass capacitors, and two 1 mm test pads. The NTC pad 1 and TP34 join `CHARGER_TS`; NTC pad 2 is grounded. U39 SETA/SETB each have their selected resistor to ground. U39 OUTA/OUTB, R91 pad 2 and U40 OE share `TEMP_OK`; R91 pad 1 and both IC supplies join `+3V3_USB`. U40 A and GND are grounded; its Y and TP35 join `CHARGER_GATE_EN`. Both bypass capacitors connect between `+3V3_USB` and GND. No fixed TS bypass was added.

The NTC is a two-terminal nonpolar part. Its project-local 1005/0402 reflow land transcribes Murata's `a=0.4`, `b=0.45`, `c=0.5 mm` NCU15 land recommendation into two 0.4 × 0.5 mm copper/mask/paste pads at ±0.425 mm, with a 1.6 × 1.2 mm first-author courtyard. Source: [Murata NCU15/NCP15 technical data and recommended land](https://www.murata.com/products/productdata/8796835971102/NTHM01E.pdf). The TMP390 six-pin symbol/land audit is [here](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/tmp390-library-audit.md); the AUP top-view pin map and DBV package follow [TI SN74AUP1G125 Rev N](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74aup1g125.pdf). These are first-author transcriptions, pending independent pin/pad and assembly review.

KiCad CLI **10.0.6** completed `./tools/check-kicad.sh` on the working tree. Native ERC reported **zero configured violations**. The complete native XML export matched **420 PCB items / 1,562 logical pins**; the dedicated temperature checker matched each of the ten components and 25 exact pin nets. The exported **15-page** PDF was inspected on page 15: all temperature symbols, labels and boundary notes are visible and legible. The source generator is deterministic, and the full check also exported the new symbol/footprint to SVG. Reproduction: run `./tools/check-kicad.sh` at the repository root. Raw run outputs were `/private/tmp/rgb-kicad-temperature-check-4/coupon-erc.rpt`, `coupon-matrix.xml` and `coupon-schematic.pdf`; SHA-256 digests respectively `4785452c3e96aeb326c2f31c049dfd5294e475d6d77d6c38d5facaf8c0bbfb95`, `2d7131beaec0b44b0214aaba604d74df757deb6cf8adc20b8e2294a6f8e196a5`, `075311446cb8187b5f2f2f6a089500cc2b60a714a1531462b32ff339b5c0ff1b`. These are reproducible temporary exports, not manufacturing files.

Open connections and qualification: `CHARGER_TS` does not yet reach the BQ24074 TS pin, and `CHARGER_GATE_EN` does not yet reach the E1 input switch EN pin. The protected pack/header, BQ timer/charge programming, E1 gate pull-up/pull-down and charger input remain to be captured. This block has no proven startup/fault behavior without those connections. The NTC and TMP390 must ultimately sit at a measured battery-facing location; test sensor-to-cell thermal error, open/short faults, trip tolerances and connector polarity before design approval. Independent Gate A review is required before fabrication.
