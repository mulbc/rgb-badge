# First charger connections on the 114 mm study, 2026-10-14

The [separate KiCad routing trial](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/paired-charger-local-routing-trial-114mm.kicad_pcb) adds five local connections to the [114 mm footprint-only board](paired-charger-114mm-placement-study-2026-10-14.md): C53 to BQ24074 U41 BAT, C54 to U41 SYS, C56 to U41 IN, R92 to U41 ISET, and R93 to U41 ILIM. It also connects both J1 VBUS contacts to E0 U45 IN and C60, E0 OUT to E1 U44 IN and C61, and E1 OUT through C52 to U41 IN and C56. C62 and C57 connect to the respective E0/E1 dVdt pins. E0's local control links join R100 to ILM, R97/R98 to OVLO, and R99 to EN; R97/R99 reach the USB input rail, while R98/R100 ground returns join the local tree. R96 and R98 also share a nearby ground return. Thus the **three nets separated by E0/E1** have staged copper, while the eFuses themselves remain electrically unqualified.

The protected BAT rail now links J2 pad 1 to C53/U41 BAT pads 2/3 and SW2 pad 5 on In1.Cu. J2 pad 2 joins the charger/USB ground tree on In2.Cu. U41 SYS pads 10/11 join C54 and SW2 pad 2 on In3.Cu. U41 exposed pad 17 joins grounded pin 6 on B.Cu without a via in the thermal pad; pins 4/6/8 connect to the partial ground tree. That tree also joins both J1 GND contacts, C60/C61/C62/C52/C57/C53/C54/C56, R92/R93/R96/R98/R100, E0/E1 pad 8 and E1 pad 2. E1 current-limit signal and connector-shell returns remain open. USB input and charger input use In4.Cu, and E0 control nets use In2.Cu. All vias are standard 0.6/0.3 mm through vias. The long BAT, SYS and header-ground trunks use 0.50 mm tracks; local links remain 0.20 mm. These are **geometry routes**, not a current-rated power design, complete ground plane or verified return-current layout. The rest of the board remains unrouted. The proposed paired-row footprints and projected drivers remain netless.

Generate the trial using KiCad's bundled Python and [the deterministic source](../../tools/generate-paired-charger-114mm-routing.py). The current export's SHA-256 is `17aea85a1846e88fb4b52df7a3c374f10e173c409fe294aa02f1d0561d847a7f`. Native KiCad CLI 10.0.6 reports the same **104 intrinsic pad-gap clearance violations** as the unrouted parent and no new geometric findings; its [raw report](paired-charger-114mm-local-routing-drc-2026-10-14.json) still caps unconnected items at 499. The full KiCad ratsnest count falls from 1,494 to 1,447 open edges, 47 fewer, with 110 trace segments and 36 through vias. The 0.50 mm inner-layer BAT branch totals 102.04 mm (including the switch spur), the ground trunk 85.23 mm and the SYS trunk 101.82 mm. The first straight BAT/ISET probe, first SYS-via position, first E1 output escape, and several initial charger/eFuse-ground via/path positions produced additional clearance findings; those paths were moved and the final trial rechecked. E0 ILM required moving its ground exit; a subsequent E1 ILM probe caused four new geometric findings and was discarded. The first J2 ground route shorted the BAT trunk/via and was detoured before this check. No hardware was built or measured.

This demonstrates that the added placement space permits charger support connections, the three USB-to-BQ rail segments, and first BAT/SYS/ground paths to the battery header and switch without new DRC geometry errors. The long single-track inner-layer trunks need a copper-weight, voltage-drop, thermal and return-loop calculation before a power layout can be selected; at nominal 35 µm copper, even 0.50 mm × 100 mm is approximately 0.10 Ω at 20 °C before vias and local necks (calculation, not measurement). This trial does **not** establish eFuse behavior, current capacity, thermal performance, row-stage correctness, battery/case fit or charging operation. Continue the remaining power routing, copper review and enclosure work before treating this as a final board.
