# First charger connections on the 114 mm study, 2026-10-14

The [separate KiCad routing trial](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/paired-charger-local-routing-trial-114mm.kicad_pcb) adds five local connections to the [114 mm footprint-only board](paired-charger-114mm-placement-study-2026-10-14.md): C53 to BQ24074 U41 BAT, C54 to U41 SYS, C56 to U41 IN, R92 to U41 ISET, and R93 to U41 ILIM. It also connects both J1 VBUS contacts to E0 U45 IN and C60, E0 OUT to E1 U44 IN and C61, and E1 OUT through C52 to U41 IN and C56. C62 and C57 connect to the respective E0/E1 dVdt pins. E0's local control links now join R100 to ILM, R97/R98 to OVLO, and R99 to EN; R97/R99 reach the USB input rail, while R98/R100 ground returns join the local tree. R96 and R98 also share a nearby ground return. Thus the **three nets separated by E0/E1** have staged copper, while the eFuses themselves remain electrically unqualified. The partial ground tree joins both J1 GND contacts, C60/C61/C62/C52/C57/C53/C54/C56, R92/R93/R96/R98/R100, U41 pads 4/6/8, E0/E1 pad 8 and E1 pad 2. U41 exposed pad 17, E1 current-limit signal and connector-shell returns remain open. The SYS path uses In3 copper, USB input and charger input use In4, the E0 control nets use In2, and the ground tree uses In2/In3, with standard 0.6/0.3 mm through vias. These are **geometry routes**, all 0.20 mm wide, not current-rated power copper or a complete return-current design. The rest of the board remains unrouted. The proposed paired-row footprints and projected drivers remain netless.

Generate the trial using KiCad's bundled Python and [the deterministic source](../../tools/generate-paired-charger-114mm-routing.py). Two consecutive exports were byte-identical, SHA-256 `60735119e23e52560144a6dddb9b1a0033525e6289dd81465b10499391616663`. Native KiCad CLI 10.0.6 reports the same **104 intrinsic pad-gap clearance violations** as the unrouted parent and no new geometric findings; its [raw report](paired-charger-114mm-local-routing-drc-2026-10-14.json) still caps unconnected items at 499. The full KiCad ratsnest count falls from 1,494 to 1,454 open edges, 40 fewer, with 98 trace segments and 31 through vias. The first straight BAT/ISET probe, first SYS-via position, first E1 output escape, and several initial charger/eFuse-ground via/path positions produced additional clearance findings; those paths were moved and the final trial rechecked. E0 ILM required moving its ground exit; a subsequent E1 ILM probe caused four new geometric findings and was discarded. No hardware was built or measured.

This demonstrates that the added placement space permits charger support connections, the three USB-to-BQ rail segments and a partial return tree without new DRC geometry errors. It does **not** establish eFuse behavior, complete ground returns, current capacity, thermal performance, row-stage correctness, battery/case fit or charging operation. Continue the remaining power returns and current-rated copper on the chosen topology and review the actual loops and enclosure before treating this as a final board.
