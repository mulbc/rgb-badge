# First charger connections on the 114 mm study, 2026-10-14

The [separate KiCad routing trial](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/paired-charger-local-routing-trial-114mm.kicad_pcb) adds five local connections to the [114 mm footprint-only board](paired-charger-114mm-placement-study-2026-10-14.md): C53 to BQ24074 U41 BAT, C54 to U41 SYS, C56 to U41 IN, R92 to U41 ISET, and R93 to U41 ILIM. The C54/SYS path uses two standard 0.6/0.3 mm through vias and In3 copper; the others use rear copper. These are **geometry routes**, all 0.20 mm wide, not current-rated power copper or complete ground returns. The rest of the board remains unrouted. The proposed paired-row footprints and projected drivers remain netless.

Generate the trial using KiCad's bundled Python and [the deterministic source](../../tools/generate-paired-charger-114mm-routing.py). Two consecutive exports were byte-identical, SHA-256 `c1173ab96ef8021c9f0e347adc886d71b1e4d1881d590190f04f00f0e1d5c3bd`. Native KiCad CLI 10.0.6 reports the same **104 intrinsic pad-gap clearance violations** as the unrouted parent and no new geometric findings; its [raw report](paired-charger-114mm-local-routing-drc-2026-10-14.json) still caps unconnected items at 499. The full KiCad ratsnest count falls from 1,494 to 1,489 open edges, exactly five fewer. The first straight BAT/ISET probe and first SYS-via position produced additional clearance findings; those paths were moved and the final trial rechecked. No hardware was built or measured.

This demonstrates that the added placement space permits several charger support connections without new DRC geometry errors. It does **not** establish USB-to-charger routing, capacitor ground returns, current capacity, thermal performance, row-stage correctness, battery/case fit or charging operation. Continue the power/USB routes on the chosen topology and review the actual current loops and enclosure before treating this as a final board.
