# First charger connections on the 114 mm study, 2026-10-14

The [separate KiCad routing trial](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/paired-charger-local-routing-trial-114mm.kicad_pcb) adds five local connections to the [114 mm footprint-only board](paired-charger-114mm-placement-study-2026-10-14.md): C53 to BQ24074 U41 BAT, C54 to U41 SYS, C56 to U41 IN, R92 to U41 ISET, and R93 to U41 ILIM. It also connects both J1 VBUS contacts to E0 U45 IN and C60, E0 OUT to E1 U44 IN and C61, and E1 OUT through C52 to U41 IN and C56. C62 and C57 connect to the respective E0/E1 dVdt pins. E0's local control links join R100 to ILM, R97/R98 to OVLO, and R99 to EN; R97/R99 reach the USB input rail, while R98/R100 ground returns join the local tree. R96 and R98 also share a nearby ground return. Thus the **three nets separated by E0/E1** have staged copper, while the eFuses themselves remain electrically unqualified.

The protected BAT rail now links J2 pad 1 to C53/U41 BAT pads 2/3 and SW2 pad 5 on In1.Cu. J2 pad 2 joins the charger/USB ground tree on In2.Cu. U41 SYS pads 10/11 join C54 and SW2 pad 2 on In3.Cu. U41 exposed pad 17 joins grounded pin 6 on B.Cu without a via in the thermal pad; pins 4/6/8 connect to the partial ground tree. That tree also joins both J1 GND contacts and all four plated shell stakes, C60/C61/C62/C52/C57/C53/C54/C56, R92/R93/R96/R98/R100, E0/E1 pad 8 and E1 pad 2. Top shell stakes join the nearby connector return on In3.Cu; bottom stakes join the charger return on In2.Cu. R96 pin 1 reaches E1 U44 ILM pin 9 by a local rear-layer escape and a detour on In4.Cu; R96 pin 2 was already tied to the ground tree. U42 logic output pin 4 reaches E1 U44 enable pin 1 on In1.Cu, and the R94/R95 pull-up/pull-down junction joins that same net. U40 and U43 inhibit outputs, plus TP35, now join the same enable node on In4.Cu. R94 pin 1 joins R77 on the USB logic supply via In2.Cu, and R95 pin 2 reaches the partial ground trunk on In2.Cu. U34 RESET pin 1 now reaches U43 inhibit input pin 1; U34 pin 6 reaches U43 supply pin 5, and each of U40/U43 has its two local ground pads joined. U17 charge-request output pin 4 now reaches U42 input pin 1 on In4.Cu, and U17/U42 share an initial USB-logic supply branch on In2.Cu. Their ground pins reach the partial ground tree; U42 pins 2/3 are also joined locally. U39 TEMP_OK pins 4/6, R91 pin 2 and U40 input pin 1 now form one routed net: a rear-layer escape under the LED field reaches a standard via beyond its right edge, then returns on In4.Cu. All three E1 inhibit inputs and outputs therefore have an initial physical path. U40/U43 ground-pad pairs also reach the partial ground trunk on In2.Cu. A rear-layer corridor between the 8.75 and 10.25 mm resistor rows joins the U34/U43, U40, R78, R91, R94/R77 and U17/U42 USB-logic supply branches. TMP390 U39 pin 5 reaches that branch through a rear-layer escape and an edge via; its ground pin 3 reaches the partial ground tree by a parallel edge escape. TPS70933 U29 output pin 5 and ground pin 2 now reach the logic bus and ground tree. Its +5V_USB input pin 1 is still unrouted, as are other USB-logic loads; this is not a completed supply distribution or a powered circuit. USB input and charger input also use In4.Cu, and E0 control nets use In2.Cu. All vias are standard 0.6/0.3 mm through vias. The long BAT, SYS and header-ground trunks use 0.50 mm tracks; local links remain 0.20 mm. These are **geometry routes**, not a current-rated power design, complete ground plane or verified return-current layout. The rest of the board remains unrouted. The proposed paired-row footprints and projected drivers remain netless.

Generate the trial using KiCad's bundled Python and [the deterministic source](../../tools/generate-paired-charger-114mm-routing.py). The current export's SHA-256 is `04a75690492e839c269841ac1fd9b7b3b68d07b8a1e962f3a54079997410378b`. Native KiCad CLI 10.0.6 reports the same **104 intrinsic pad-gap clearance violations** as the unrouted parent and no new geometric findings; its [raw report](paired-charger-114mm-local-routing-drc-2026-10-14.json) still caps unconnected items at 499. The full KiCad ratsnest count falls from 1,494 to 1,411 open edges, 83 fewer, with 220 trace segments and 64 through vias. The 0.50 mm inner-layer BAT branch totals 102.04 mm (including the switch spur), the ground trunk 85.23 mm and the SYS trunk 101.82 mm. The 2026-10-15 charger-permission extension connects the three inhibit outputs, their common bias and TP35; it also joins the U34 RESET output to U43 input, a local U34/U43 USB-logic supply branch and each U40/U43 ground-pad pair. The first straight BAT/ISET probe, first SYS-via position, first E1 output escape, and several initial charger/eFuse-ground via/path positions produced additional clearance findings; those paths were moved and the final trial rechecked. E0 ILM required moving its ground exit. The first E1 ILM via positions collided with ground/OVLO, BAT or SYS copper; the retained detour clears those features under the current DRC settings. The first U42 enable via intersected the projected second driver; the retained via clears that footprint. The first J2 ground route shorted the BAT trunk/via and was detoured before this check. No hardware was built or measured.

This demonstrates that the added placement space permits charger support connections, the three USB-to-BQ rail segments, and first BAT/SYS/ground paths to the battery header and switch without new DRC geometry errors. The long single-track inner-layer trunks need a copper-weight, voltage-drop, thermal and return-loop calculation before a power layout can be selected; at nominal 35 µm copper, even 0.50 mm × 100 mm is approximately 0.10 Ω at 20 °C before vias and local necks (calculation, not measurement). This trial does **not** establish eFuse behavior, current capacity, thermal performance, row-stage correctness, battery/case fit or charging operation. Continue the remaining power routing, copper review and enclosure work before treating this as a final board.
