# Native 114 mm grouped placement study, 2026-10-14

The [generated KiCad board](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/paired-charger-placement-only-114mm.kicad_pcb) applies the 114 × 32.5 mm sensitivity in [ADR 0033](../decisions/0033-badge-length-relaxation-proposal.md) to actual component lands and courtyards. It is a **footprint-only, unrouted comparison**, not the canonical schematic's PCB. The generator is [here](../../tools/generate-paired-charger-114mm-placement.py).

The study retains the current coupon source's 256 front LEDs and 209 rear footprints. It removes the 32 separate row transistors and adds sixteen **netless** project-local `DMC1229UFDB-7` candidate pair lands plus two **netless** projected final-driver lands: 483 footprints total, including 227 on the rear. The retained footprints preserve their schematic pad nets, but the board has no tracks, vias or zones. The front coupon grid is shifted 3 mm left from the 120 mm board; this is **not** a final 48 × 16 LED grid. The battery and sensor regions are XY reservations; the cell, mount, insulation, case, USB cutout, RF clearance and assembly stack are absent. U38 moves 0.1 mm inward from its first-fit box center to meet the existing 0.5 mm board-edge copper clearance in this trial.

The board generated twice with identical SHA-256 `037c17c2c729495af7a4956cd913d6178d4c78a203f0ff42dc04137263b518e9`. KiCad CLI **10.0.6** loaded it and produced the [raw DRC report](paired-charger-114mm-placement-drc-2026-10-14.json): **104 geometric clearance violations**, all intrinsic to existing lands under the current 0.20 mm global rule—six per proposed pair package (96 total) and four each in U48 and U52 (eight total). There are no other reported geometric violations. The CLI lists **499 unconnected items**, its cap for this overwhelmingly unrouted board; this is not a connection count or electrical pass. The known pair-land gap is 0.15 mm, and no design-rule exception or assembler process has been approved.

This result clears an actual-pad **placement geometry** hurdle for the 114 mm study. It does not establish row-pair numeric pin mapping, connected power/standby/charger behavior, copper routing, display escape, current capacity, thermal performance, battery clearance, USB/case fit or fabrication readiness. The next useful board step is to qualify the exact pair pin map or retain the existing separate row circuit, then route a chosen topology while preserving the hardware-default dark state. MEC-001's 110 mm outer-case maximum remains in force until case tradeoff and fit review.
