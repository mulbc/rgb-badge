# Native 114 mm grouped placement study, 2026-10-14

The [generated KiCad board](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/paired-charger-placement-only-114mm.kicad_pcb) applies the 114 × 32.5 mm sensitivity in [ADR 0033](../decisions/0033-badge-length-relaxation-proposal.md) to actual component lands and courtyards. It is a **footprint-only, unrouted comparison**, not the canonical schematic's PCB. The generator is [here](../../tools/generate-paired-charger-114mm-placement.py).

The study retains the current coupon source's 256 front LEDs and 209 rear footprints. It removes the 32 separate row transistors and adds sixteen **netless** project-local `DMC1229UFDB-7` candidate pair lands plus two **netless** projected final-driver lands: 483 footprints total, including 227 on the rear. The retained footprints preserve their schematic pad nets, but the board has no tracks, vias or zones. The front coupon grid is shifted 3 mm left from the 120 mm board; this is **not** a final 48 × 16 LED grid. The LP452845 body and sensor region are XY reservations; the cell, mount, insulation, case, USB cutout, RF clearance and assembly stack are absent. U38 moves 0.1 mm inward from its first-fit box center to meet the existing 0.5 mm board-edge copper clearance in this trial.

The original free first-fit left the battery-facing NTC TH1 at the pack edge and TMP390 U39 away from the pack. The corrected generator **fixes both actual footprints inside the 10 × 8 mm central sensor reservation**: TH1 at (54.375, 16.125) mm, about 0.18 mm from the projected 47 × 28.5 mm pack-body centre, and U39 at (57.625, 15.5) mm. With these two fixed boxes and the nine trial-near interlock bypass boxes, the 114 mm priority placement still has zero missing components; a 112 mm run leaves C70 unplaced. The move is conditional on sensor-to-cell thermal coupling, electrical insulation, pouch swelling clearance and actual U39/TH1 assembly height. It is not a validated temperature measurement or battery stack.

The corrected board generated twice with identical SHA-256 `d675c210489c000714cd315935e6ce5e3c13ec8f35a40a23efa62e0444d61352`. KiCad CLI **10.0.6** loaded it and produced the [raw DRC report](paired-charger-114mm-placement-drc-2026-10-14.json): **104 geometric clearance violations**, all intrinsic to existing lands under the current 0.20 mm global rule—six per proposed pair package (96 total) and four each in U48 and U52 (eight total). There are no other reported geometric violations. The CLI lists **499 unconnected items**, its cap for this overwhelmingly unrouted board; this is not a connection count or electrical pass. The known pair-land gap is 0.15 mm, and no design-rule exception or assembler process has been approved.

This result clears an actual-pad **placement geometry** hurdle for the 114 mm study. It does not establish row-pair numeric pin mapping, connected power/standby/charger behavior, copper routing, display escape, current capacity, thermal performance, battery clearance, USB/case fit or fabrication readiness. The next useful board step is to qualify the exact pair pin map or retain the existing separate row circuit, then route a chosen topology while preserving the hardware-default dark state. ADR 0033 and revised MEC-001 adopt 118 mm as the outer-case length limit; the case has not been modeled or fitted.
