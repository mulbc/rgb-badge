# Output-isolated display interlock capture: first-author review

Date: 2026-10-12. Source branch: `coupon-power-rev-a`. This is a **schematic source review**, not a hardware measurement or a fabrication release. The parent design decision is [ADR 0032](../decisions/0032-two-event-display-interlock-proposal.md); the [circuit study](../design/display-interlock-circuit-proposal-2026-10-12.md) describes the intended state behavior.

The new root-linked page places all nine exact ICs, nine 100 nF bypass capacitors, eleven exact resistors and two 1 mm candidate-output test pads. Controller GPIO15/pad 8 now joins `DISPLAY_HEARTBEAT`; GPIO16/pad 9 joins `DISPLAY_ARM_CLK`. The source connects watchdog RESET, inverter, two separate flip-flop clocks, asynchronous clears, comparator dividers and wired-AND window outputs, row-request inverter/final NAND, and the candidate VLED/row arm gates. Pin-level values and net names are guarded by [the deterministic source checker](../../tools/check-coupon-display-interlock.py) and the complete native XML checker. The sheet is generated by [the interlock source generator](../../tools/generate-coupon-display-interlock.py).

**The display remains disabled.** `VLED_ENABLE_CANDIDATE` ends at TP13 and `ROW_ENABLE_N_CANDIDATE` at TP14. Neither net joins the actual converter EN or decoder E. Native XML still shows U37.12 and R87.1 alone on `VLED_ENABLE_DRAFT`, and U2.23 and R42.2 alone on `ROW_ENABLE_N`. The source checker fails if either candidate output bypasses this separation.

KiCad CLI 10.0.6 exported and checked the complete 20-page schematic: **zero configured ERC violations**, **497 PCB items / 1,814 logical pins**. The checker confirms 9 exact interlock ICs, 20 support parts and 2 candidate test pads and validates the key heartbeat, arm, clear, voltage-window, row and dark-boundary pin assignments. The complete page-20 PDF was visually inspected after spreading the eleven resistors over two rows to remove label crowding. The schematic uses labels to show each net; PCB placement and routing have not begun.

The comparator uses the newer DSE0006A 4220552/B land, with its mask choice awaiting assembler review. The first [fit screen](../../mechanical/display-interlock-body-fit-2026-10-12.md) contained nine ICs and twenty 0402 support boxes but omitted the two new test pads. The [captured-courtyard screen](../../mechanical/captured-interlock-fit-2026-10-12.md) counts all 31 parts and leaves 20 greedy misses at 108 mm with the present 32 row FETs. An unselected dual-package row trial has zero misses there, still without grouped placement, routing or case walls. It therefore cannot certify the 108 mm target.

Before connecting TP13/TP14 nets to the actual outputs, review the slow ESP_EN RC input and GPIO0 mode strap, the clear path at all supply ramps, watchdog timing and stalled-firmware behavior, comparator threshold/hysteresis/ripple corners, actual row-off response with retained VLED, and a grouped placement with all 31 parts. The first physical board then needs fault injection and rail/row waveforms; independent Gate A review remains mandatory before fabrication. No cell, board or enclosure has been physically tested.
