# Provisional E0 protected-USB capture, 2026-10-11

The [root-linked E0 sheet](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/usb-protection.kicad_sch) represents [ADR 0027](../decisions/0027-e0-protected-usb-source-selection.md). Exact TPS259474ARPWR U45 connects `VBUS_CONNECTOR` to `+5V_USB`, which feeds the USB logic and E1 IN. E0 EN is fed autonomously from connector VBUS through 390 kΩ. A 38.3 kΩ/10 kΩ divider sets OVLO; 3.32 kΩ sets ILM; 2.2 nF C0G sets dVdt; ITIMER, PG and PGTH are explicitly unconnected. Exact TDK 1 µF/50 V X7R at IN and Murata 22 µF/10 V X5R at OUT are local schematic components. The former fictitious protected-rail source flags have been removed; #FLG05 now represents the external connector supply on raw VBUS for ERC only.

The TPS259474 pin functions and RPW0010A land pattern reuse the [manufacturer-controlled audit](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/input-protection-library-audit.md). The two new E0 resistor symbols inherit the audited passive ERJ2 pins and 0402 land; the other two resistor symbols already existed. The new TDK input capacitor has a separate [exact land audit](../../hardware/coupon/rev-a/tdk-e0-capacitor-library-audit.md); its 0.7 mm pad/gap geometry was checked against the manufacturer reflow table. The source checker compares eight exact E0 values/footprints, all 24 native pin nets and the connector/logic/E1 joins. No substitution is permitted without updating the decision and audits.

First-author source validation used KiCad CLI 10.0.6 on macOS. Full `tools/check-kicad.sh` completed controlled library exports, zero configured ERC violations, and full native XML matching **447 PCB items and 1,654 logical pins**. The PDF exported **18 pages**; changed pages 17–18 were rendered and visually inspected for readable values, nets, pin numbers, NC marks and titles. Focused raw evidence: `/private/tmp/rgb-e0-first-erc-20261011.rpt`, `/private/tmp/rgb-e0-first-20261011.xml`, `/private/tmp/rgb-e0-review-20261011.pdf`, `/private/tmp/rgb-e0-page17.png`, `/private/tmp/rgb-e0-page18.png`, and `/private/tmp/rgb-e0-full-check-20261011.log`. These are source/electrical-rule checks, **not** measurements or simulation. The durable evidence is the canonical KiCad source, deterministic generator, checkers and this record.

The full host suite passed **171 tests** on 2026-10-11, including deterministic USB-sheet regeneration and E0 output-pin fault injection. The initial full run found a stale USB-sheet generator note and source flag; those were corrected before the passing rerun. `git diff --check` was clean.

No PCB, supply, cable, pack or firmware has been tested. The 5.620–6.006 V rising OVLO and 5.112–5.481 V falling ranges remain **static calculations** under the stated resistor/leakage assumptions. TI's 0.850–1.150 A ILM row is a device test point, not a guaranteed complete USB source limit. E0 can admit a connector transient before OVLO disconnects; line inductance, input/output capacitor effective values, surge suppression, fault overshoot, auto-retry, reverse energy, attach inrush and whole-port current need engineering and bench qualification. E1 permission/startup/temperature behavior, pack connection and independent Gate A review remain open. This source capture does not authorize fabrication.
