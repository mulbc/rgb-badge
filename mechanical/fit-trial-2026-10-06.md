# Final-badge board and battery fit trial

The [rear plan](review/fit-trial-plan.svg), [front LED/USB screen](review/placement.svg), [11 mm section](review/fit-trial-section.svg) and [machine-readable positions](review/fit-trial.json) are an early, to-scale fit trial. Rebuild the rear and section artifacts from the current native KiCad coupon netlist and project-local footprint courtyards with:

```sh
python3 tools/screen-final-fit-trial.py
```

This is **not a KiCad PCB**, routing/DRC, complete 48 × 16 netlist, approved battery mount or case CAD. It is a deliberate test of the current 106 × 32.5 mm board and 110 × 35 × 11 mm case targets before changing requirements. The [LP503055 manufacturer drawing](https://www.lipolbattery.com/LiPo-Battery-Datahseet/LiPo_Battery_LP503055_3.7V_950mAh.pdf) gives a maximum finished body of 56 × 30.5 × 5.3 mm for its bare-wire configuration. It is a separate, wired pack that **must be supported by the enclosure above the PCB**; neither that support, a keyed connector, nor the final local shape of the protection board, wire bend and strain relief is available.

## Plan result

The trial places the ESP32 module at X 0.5–20.1 mm, Y 9.25–29.75 mm, shifts the previously screened three TLC59581 bodies to the right, and puts the existing row decoder, switch/button footprints and all 32 row-MOSFET courtyards into side/top strips. It gives 77 currently captured 0402 resistors/capacitors nonoverlapping **XY candidate positions beneath the pack**, including the row-gate resistor pairs near its driver-side edge. The 4 × 4 mm centre box remains a *target reservation* for the future battery NTC, not its selected land pattern. Courtyards of these anchored parts do not intersect in this projection.

The rest of the captured coupon parts are then tried in the remaining rear rectangles with zero *additional* gap beyond their project-local courtyards. **Eight currently captured parts remain without a position:** U11, U17, U23, U24, U25, U29, U34 and U33 (USB conditioning/permission, logic supply and supervisor). Other automatically assigned parts are only geometric trial positions; their nets, decoupler locality, thermal grouping and routability are unreviewed. The exact top-edge actuator directions for the provisional SW1/SW2 footprints also remain open.

Even this optimistic plan omits major required or proposed final hardware. The project-local courtyards of just the proposed BQ24074 charger IC, TPS63020 LED-supply IC, two TPS259474 input switches and optional INA232 monitor total **64.90 mm²** before their passives, inductors, thermal copper or connections. The keyed pack connector, cable path, gauge isolation, display interlock, two added driver support networks, antenna/cable zone and two M2 mounting features have no assigned location. These are unplaced needs or candidate allowances, not permission to crowd the existing parts or omit required functions.

The [battery-connector screen](battery-connector-screen-2026-10-06.md) evaluates a provisional JST PH side-entry assembly. An illustrative 8.0 × 9.6 mm mated pocket has zero collision-free positions in either orientation **even before** automatic placement of the remaining captured parts. Its actual land pattern, cable direction and wire clearance are not selected. This calls for rearranging the grouped components with a connector reserved first; it does not prove the board outline impossible.

## Section result

The [existing envelope trial](envelope-screen-2026-09-26.md) allocates 4.9 mm outside the pack: 0.6 diffuser, 0.5 optical gap, 1.2 front LED height, 0.8 PCB, 0.3 electrical isolation, 0.5 swelling/installation allowance and 1.0 rear shell. Adding the LP503055's 5.3 mm maximum body gives **10.2 mm** before a rear PCB component. For an illustrative **0.55 mm** 0402 MLCC height—shown for the project's GRM155 part in a [Murata manufacturer BOM](https://www.murata.com/-/media/webrenewal/products/connectivitymodule/asset/pub/rfm/data/type2nr/type2nr-evk-bom.ashx?cvid=20250613041846000000&la=en)—the sum is **10.75 mm**, leaving only **0.25 mm nominal** in an 11 mm case.

That 0.25 mm is **not qualified clearance**. Assembled heights, solder, film or rigid cover, adhesive, supports, cell swelling, wall/diffuser tolerances and the thermal delay/error of the board NTC are not bounded by this arithmetic. There is no approved mount that guarantees the pack never presses on a component or solder joint. The 77 under-pack XY positions therefore remain conditional and cannot be transferred blindly to a PCB.

## Disposition

The current outline **does not yet pass a complete fit trial**: eight existing parts and all remaining power/connector/RF/mounting needs are unplaced, while the under-pack section has little nominal height margin. This does not prove that a skilled layout or revised case stack cannot fit. It does show that the earlier generic first-fit count was not the right decision metric, and that no reduction in display resolution or safety clearance is justified from it.

The next electrical input needed for a release-grade layout is a complete power netlist and exact connector/antenna choices. In parallel, the enclosure trial should specify a real insulating battery support and measure the full height/tolerance stack with inert pack mockups. Then a KiCad PCB can place the complete netlist, run DRC, and go through independent Gate A review. No board or pack has been physically fitted or ordered.
