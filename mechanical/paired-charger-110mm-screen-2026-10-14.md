# Grouped power and paired-row 110 mm XY screen, 2026-10-14

The [deterministic candidate screen](../tools/screen-paired-charger-110mm-xy.py) uses the current 497-item coupon KiCad XML and actual project-local courtyards. It replaces the 32 separately captured row transistors with **sixteen unapproved `DMC1229UFDB-7` 3 × 3 mm courtyard boxes** for placement only. The canonical schematic, BOM and routed 120 mm board still use the 32 original devices. The result is an [inspectable SVG](review/paired-charger-110mm-xy.svg) and [full box manifest](review/paired-charger-110mm-xy.json).

On a **110 × 32.5 mm** XY board, this particular first-fit ordering places **227 rear boxes with zero misses**. Those include 225 candidate rear components plus two extra projected final-badge driver bodies; the 256 coupon LEDs are counted in source but remain on the opposite face. The trial reserves the selected LP452845 maximum 47 × 28.5 mm battery body and a 10 × 8 mm central sensing region. It explicitly groups E0/E1, C60/C61/C62/C52/C57, five E0/E1 programming resistors, BQ24074 U41, C53/C54/C56 and R92/R93 at the USB end. The two battery/SYS capacitors are 0.5 mm courtyard-gap left of U41, R92 is 1.0 mm left, and C56 is 0.5 mm below. The five USB programming resistors are within 0.5–1.0 mm courtyard gap of their assigned eFuse body in this XY pattern. These are rectangle separations, not trace lengths or validated electrical locality.

The ordinary area-first ordering leaves U50 without a box. Giving U39 and U50 priority before the remaining large items yields zero misses. A simple 108 mm sensitivity, shifting this USB/charger group and the three projected drivers 2 mm left, leaves six boxes unplaced in ordinary area-first order and nine in priority order. This does not prove a different 108 mm packing is impossible; it shows that this 110 mm solution has no demonstrated length margin. **118 small parts are assigned under the battery body**, conditional on insulation, vertical height, wire exit and heat. The nominal J1 courtyard extends 0.75 mm beyond the drawn right PCB edge as a mid-mount projection; no connector cutout or edge tolerance is represented. The battery sensor components are counted but not forced into the central reserved box. Nine display-interlock bypass capacitors are not constrained near their ICs. The front LED matrix, actual copper, vias, thermal loops, RF keepout, mounting and 11 mm enclosure stack are also absent.

This is a useful space direction: the current 120 mm staged board cannot accommodate nearby C53/C54/R92 with 32 separate row transistors at its checked spacing, while this **provisional paired-row 110 mm XY arrangement** holds the missing charger parts and five USB resistors locally without dropping a box. It is **not a routeable-board or finished-case proof**. Before choosing the pair part, independently verify its exact numbered pin map, the package's 0.15 mm land gap and assembly process, hot row-current/thermal behavior, and default-dark switching. Then build and DRC a 110 mm board with the grouped parts, reconnect its source nets, and check the actual connector cutout, stack-up, bypass loops and enclosure. Independent Gate A remains required before fabrication.
