# Conditional dual-row package XY screen

The [to-scale rear plan](review/dual-row-package-plan.svg), [magnified converter bay](review/vled-cluster-detail.svg) and [machine-readable positions](review/fit-trial.json) test the geometry consequence of [proposed ADR 0020](../docs/decisions/0020-dual-row-mosfet-package-proposal.md). Regenerate them with `python3 tools/screen-final-fit-trial.py`. This is an **unrouted package-area screen**, not a new schematic, footprint, selected battery termination or qualified PCB fit.

The starting point is the [connector-first trial](connector-first-trial-2026-10-06.md): 106 × 32.5 mm board, centred 56 × 30.5 mm LP503055 projected body, illustrative 8 × 8 mm Molex connector pocket, relocated switch and decoder, three projected driver bodies and 77 conditional 0402 positions below a separately supported pack. This screen removes only the **32 original row MOSFET courtyards** from that trial and reserves sixteen **3 × 3 mm candidate courtyards** for `DMC1229UFDB-7` complementary N/P pairs, read from the [first-author project footprint](../hardware/coupon/rev-a/dual-row-package-library-audit.md). It retains every other captured coupon part. A tighter trial moves decoder `U2` to the upper-right edge, freeing the lower-right strip for the thirteen parts of the [unlinked VLED converter](../hardware/coupon/rev-a/vled-converter-capture-contract.md). It also reserves one BQ24074 and two TPS259474 IC courtyards. [ADR 0021](../docs/decisions/0021-external-battery-current-measurement.md) removes the originally planned INA232 monitor/shunt; its IC box is included only in a historical comparison. These IC-package minima are **not complete power circuits**.

The VLED cluster occupies an 11 × 9.3 mm envelope at x=91–102 mm, y=22.8–32.1 mm. Its IC courtyard is x=91–95.9 mm, y=25.5–29.4 mm. The rotated inductor is directly to the right, bridging the IC's switching-pin sides; first input capacitor `C44` and first output capacitor `C46` are on their respective pin sides, each 2.974 mm centre-to-centre from the IC. The farthest extra output capacitor is 7.558 mm from the IC centre. These are courtyard-centre distances, **not routed loop lengths or pad-to-pad clearances**. A [separate native KiCad placement](../hardware/coupon/rev-a/staging/vled-layout-trial.kicad_pcb) now instantiates the thirteen actual project footprints and exports the staged schematic nets to their pads. It has no neighbouring final-board circuitry, tracks, vias or zones. KiCad loads and plots it, but PCB DRC aborts in this environment, including on a one-footprint control file; no DRC pass is claimed. [TI's layout guidance](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps63020.pdf) calls for short, wide power paths, nearby input/output capacitors and inductor, a quiet control-ground/feedback path, and separated power/control ground regions. This trial has not met those instructions yet.

All drawn XY boxes are nonoverlapping, sometimes with **zero added gap**. The decoder move remains a layout variant; ADR 0021 separately accepted the monitor omission after retaining external measurement evidence. The INA232 comparison proves only that this first-fit order loses one pair box when its IC courtyard is restored; a better whole-board placement might fit it. The candidate pair land geometry was transcribed from the manufacturer Type B package file, but its pin numbering, paste/mask, mounting and actual routing have not passed independent review. The proposal still omits charger/input-support passives and inductors, gauge buffering, hardware display interlock, two extra driver support networks, USB/RF and battery cable routes, thermal copper, assembly access and full 11 mm section tolerances. The LP503055 is still an unselected terminated-pack candidate; its cell/body drawing does not settle protection, connector polarity or wire bend. None of those omissions can be inferred to fit from this drawing.

**Disposition:** the tighter VLED grouping is a useful starting location for a real PCB placement, but the full circuit cannot yet be credited as fitted. ADR 0021 removes live signed current telemetry while preserving external coupon/final current logs. Independently check the candidate row-package pin/land geometry and bound final-row current and compact-board heat, then decide whether the coupon should carry the new row stage. Continue the complete coupon power circuit and exact-pack search in parallel. The manufacturer's [dual MOSFET datasheet](https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/DMC1229UFDB.pdf) supplies package and electrical screening values; the 3 × 3 mm courtyard is a project placement allowance.
