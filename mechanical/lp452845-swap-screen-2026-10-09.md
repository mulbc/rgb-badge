# LP452845 connectorized-pack swap screen

The [LiPol finished-assembly drawing FD_6225_10, index 1](https://www.lipobattery.us/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/LP452845-3.7V-900mAh-with-JST-PHR-2.pdf) specifies a protected `LP452845` with two AWG 26 leads and JST `PHR-2`, no integrated NTC. Its maximum drawn body is **47 × 28.5 × 4.8 mm**, compared with **56 × 30.5 × 5.3 mm** for the protected, bare-wire `LP503055`. This corrects the earlier sourcing note that no finished drawing was available. The [model page](https://www.lipobattery.us/900mah-lipo-battery-lp452845-3-7v-900mah-3-33wh-with-protection-circuit-and-wires-50mm-and-jst-phr-2/) says five-piece samples may ship in a week **when stocked**; it does not establish live stock, price or a purchase-ready order.

The [reproducible geometric comparison](review/lp452845-swap.json) centres the new maximum body on the same 106 × 32.5 mm board and reuses the existing October 6 anchor rectangles. It is a swap screen, not a new packed PCB:

| Result | Existing LP503055 | LP452845 swap |
|---|---:|---:|
| Maximum body projection | 56 × 30.5 mm | 47 × 28.5 mm |
| Maximum body thickness | 5.3 mm | 4.8 mm |
| Outside-body board area recovered | — | 368.5 mm² |
| Illustrative case-section total with 0402 beneath pack | 10.75 mm | 10.25 mm |
| Nominal unused height in 11 mm case | 0.25 mm | 0.75 mm |
| Existing 77 under-pack 0402 XY positions still inside the body | 77 | 20; **57 need moving** |
| Free 8 × 9.6 or 9.6 × 8 mm PH pocket positions with old anchors | 0 | 0 |

The shorter body improves nominal height and area, but it does not make the **original grouped placement** fit a PH connector. In the separate, still-proposed [dual-row-package variant](dual-row-package-screen-2026-10-06.md), the former 8 × 8 mm left-end pocket can expand to an illustrative **9.6 × 8 mm PH pocket** at x=16.5–26.1, y=0.5–8.5 mm. It clears the new pack body and all existing fixed/other trial courtyards. Moving 57 of the 77 conditional under-pack 0402 boxes gives all 77 nonoverlapping XY positions inside the new body projection while retaining 20 old positions and the battery-facing NTC reservation. The exact positions are in the [JSON](review/lp452845-swap.json). This shows one possible *geometric* repack; it does not approve the dual-row electrical substitution or establish a routed board.

The PH pocket is an illustrative mated-area rectangle, not an audited JST land pattern. Connector lands, housing direction, lead bend, strain relief, insulating support, swelling tolerance, heat and copper/routing are still absent. The actual connector polarity must be checked on the terminated assembly. No physical pack, board or case has been measured.

Electrically, the drawing gives **900 mAh minimum**, **450 mA maximum charge**, **900 mA maximum discharge** and a **+10 to +45 °C charge range**. The proposed 2.49 kΩ ISET upper calculation of 396 mA is 54 mA below the listed charge ceiling, conditional on total resistor error and pack qualification. The reference model gives 0.9 Ah × 3.7 V × 85% / 0.45 W = **6.29 h idealized**; this is only 0.29 h above the six-hour target before measured variation. The existing 2.49 W full-white model gives 0.83 A at 3.0 V, only 0.07 A below the pack's *maximum* discharge statement, which is **not explicitly a continuous rating**. Therefore `LP452845` is a strong *connectorized fit candidate*, but it is not yet a final pack selection or a justification to freeze the charger and layout. The board-facing NTC remains required by ADR 0019 and must be tested against cell-surface temperature; the +10 °C lower charge bound is material to its hardware trip setting.

Source data and calculations are recorded in [the dated sourcing update](../docs/sourcing/pack-screen-2026-10-05.md). The geometric script reads the saved [October 6 fit trial](review/fit-trial.json); it does not rerun the native KiCad inventory or optimize electrical locality.
