# LP452845 connectorized-pack swap screen

The [LiPol finished-assembly drawing FD_6225_10, index 1](https://www.lipobattery.us/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/LP452845-3.7V-900mAh-with-JST-PHR-2.pdf) specifies a protected `LP452845` with two AWG 26 leads and JST `PHR-2`, no integrated NTC. Its maximum drawn body is **47 × 28.5 × 4.8 mm**, compared with **56 × 30.5 × 5.3 mm** for the protected, bare-wire `LP503055`. This corrects the earlier sourcing note that no finished drawing was available. The [model page](https://www.lipobattery.us/900mah-lipo-battery-lp452845-3-7v-900mah-3-33wh-with-protection-circuit-and-wires-50mm-and-jst-phr-2/) says five-piece samples may ship in a week **when stocked**; it does not establish live stock, price or a purchase-ready order.

The [reproducible geometric comparison](review/lp452845-swap.json) shifts the new maximum body **1.5 mm right of centre** on the same 106 × 32.5 mm board and reuses the existing October 6 anchor rectangles. It is a swap screen, not a new packed PCB:

| Result | Existing LP503055 | LP452845 swap |
|---|---:|---:|
| Maximum body projection | 56 × 30.5 mm | 47 × 28.5 mm |
| Maximum body thickness | 5.3 mm | 4.8 mm |
| Outside-body board area recovered | — | 368.5 mm² |
| Illustrative case-section total with 0402 beneath pack | 10.75 mm | 10.25 mm |
| Nominal unused height in 11 mm case | 0.25 mm | 0.75 mm |
| Existing 77 under-pack 0402 XY positions still inside the body | 77 | 34; **43 need moving** |
| Free 8 × 9.6 or 9.6 × 8 mm PH pocket positions with old anchors | 0 | 0 |

The shorter body improves nominal height and area, but it does not make the **original grouped placement** fit a PH connector. The earlier 9.6 × 8 mm rectangle was also too shallow: [JST's side-entry SMT board layout](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/ePH.pdf) calls for **9 mm minimum depth**. In the separate, still-proposed [dual-row-package variant](dual-row-package-screen-2026-10-06.md), the KiCad 10.0.6 `S2B-PH-SM4-TB` footprint has a **9.2 × 10.2 mm courtyard**, placed at x=20.25–29.45, y=0.25–10.45 mm. The shifted pack begins at x=31.0 mm, leaving **1.55 mm XY** between body and connector courtyard. Captured 0805 output capacitor `C42` moves to x=16.5–20.25, y=0.25–2.25 mm. Moving 43 of the 77 conditional under-pack 0402 boxes gives all 77 nonoverlapping XY positions inside the shifted pack projection while retaining 34 old positions and moving the battery-facing sensor reservation with the pack. The exact positions are in the [JSON](review/lp452845-swap.json). This shows one possible *geometric* repack; it does not approve the dual-row electrical substitution or establish a routed board.

The revised screen reserves a **10 × 8 mm battery-facing sensor cluster** at x=49.5–59.5, y=12.25–20.25 mm. Eight nonoverlapping trial boxes represent the Murata BQ TS NTC, `TMP390A2DRLR`, its two threshold resistors and 10 kΩ output pull-up, two exact Murata bypass capacitors, and the second AUP inhibit gate. All 77 earlier under-pack 0402 courtyards still fit outside that cluster in this XY screen. The TMP390 box is a conservative 2.7 × 2.5 mm placeholder until its project-local DRL0006A land is drawn and audited; the screen does not establish routability, sensor thermal coupling, or space for mounting adhesive and insulation.

The connector footprint courtyard is a more useful anchor than the old illustrative pocket, but its solder lands and pin-one mark still require independent comparison with JST's mounting-side drawing before board capture. The mated housing, lead bend, strain relief, insulating support, swelling tolerance, heat, C42 electrical locality and copper/routing are still absent. The actual connector polarity must be checked on the terminated assembly. No physical pack, board or case has been measured.

Electrically, the drawing gives **900 mAh minimum**, **450 mA maximum charge**, **900 mA maximum discharge** and a **+10 to +45 °C charge range**. [ADR 0024](../docs/decisions/0024-lp452845-pack-and-temperature-window.md) now selects this exact terminated assembly as the Rev A **design target**. The proposed 2.49 kΩ ISET upper calculation of 396 mA is 54 mA below the listed charge ceiling, conditional on total resistor error and pack qualification. The reference model gives 0.9 Ah × 3.7 V × 85% / 0.45 W = **6.29 h idealized**; this is only 0.29 h above the six-hour target before measured variation. The existing 2.49 W full-white model gives 0.83 A at 3.0 V, only 0.07 A below the pack's *maximum* discharge statement, which is **not explicitly a continuous rating**. The chosen battery-facing NTC and additional hardware hot/cold guard must be tested against cell-surface temperature before the pack, charger and layout can be frozen.

Source data and calculations are recorded in [the dated sourcing update](../docs/sourcing/pack-screen-2026-10-05.md). The geometric script reads the saved [October 6 fit trial](review/fit-trial.json); it does not rerun the native KiCad inventory or optimize electrical locality.
