# Display-interlock IC-body fit stress screen

Date: 2026-10-12. Historical calculation from the pre-interlock-capture coupon KiCad netlist at commit `ba824cb`, **not** a PCB, footprint placement, enclosure fit, or hardware measurement. The raw result is [display-interlock-body-fit-2026-10-12.json](review/display-interlock-body-fit-2026-10-12.json), generated by [screen-display-interlock-fit.py](../tools/screen-display-interlock-fit.py). The source XML SHA-256 is `4794253497d78b0c9d7e278cc80d91dcacd7eea7246e1503ea479db2f7fe0020`; it contains 466 coupon PCB items and 207 rear-screened parts. To reproduce this historical screen, export that revision's schematic with KiCad 10.0.6 and pass its XML to the script. The current revision now includes the captured interlock and requires the [captured-courtyard screen](captured-interlock-fit-2026-10-12.md).

The screen carries forward the LP452845 47 × 28.5 mm pack body, 10 × 8 mm sensor reservation, fixed connector/switch/decoder/MCU/three-driver projections and the *proposed* 16 dual row-package substitution from the [current XY trial](current-lp452845-xy-screen-2026-10-11.md). It adds the nine candidate IC package bodies named by the [interlock proposal](../docs/design/display-interlock-circuit-proposal-2026-10-12.md). Their nominal body areas sum to **41.01 mm²** before lands, passives, routing and clearance. Package body dimensions come from the corresponding [TI supervisor](https://www.ti.com/product/TPS3823), [flip-flop](https://www.ti.com/product/SN74LVC1G74), [comparator](https://www.ti.com/product/TLV6700), [NAND](https://www.ti.com/product/SN74LVC2G00), [AND](https://www.ti.com/product/SN74LVC2G08), [inverter](https://www.ti.com/product/SN74LVC1G04) and [3-input AND](https://www.ti.com/product/SN74LVC1G11) product pages. The compact case floors every proposed IC to a project-local courtyard.

The existing first-fit engine adds 0.5 mm to each candidate box dimension and snaps to a 0.25 mm grid. This stress screen adds another 0.5 or 1.0 mm per body dimension to approximate land/courtyard demand, then reruns the existing area-first heuristic. Where a local land exists, the screen also uses its actual courtyard as a minimum: 4.3 × 3.6 mm for the TI DBV five-pin supervisor, 2.4 × 2.4 mm for each compact TI RSE flip-flop, 1.9 × 1.5 mm for each DSF logic IC, 3.7 × 3.7 mm for each DCU dual gate, 2.5 × 2.1 mm for DSE and 1.6 × 2.0 mm for DRY. The [four-land audit](../hardware/coupon/rev-a/display-interlock-library-audit.md) records their drawing sources, revision choice and native exports.

The IC-body result is more restrictive than reserving one empty 7 × 12 mm rectangle: the actual proposal distributes nine ICs and cannot assume they pack into that single box. These first six runs omit the interlock resistors, capacitors, test access and routing, while the case limit is 110 mm *outside*, with no wall or USB-opening allowance modeled. Thus none of these widths is approved. The practical next work is to build a grouped placement with support passives, then check case section and wiring. A fitted heuristic is not evidence that PCB routing or assembly will work.

An additional **package-only** sensitivity keeps all nine logic functions but changes both flops to `SN74LVC1G74RSER` UQFN, the inverter to `SN74LVC1G04DSFR` X2SON, the 3-input AND to `SN74LVC1G11DRYR` USON and the 2-input AND to `SN74LVC1G08DSFR` X2SON. TI lists these exact orderable packages; their nominal IC-body sum falls to **27.24 mm²**. This does not change the circuit decision or approve sub-0.5 mm-pitch assembly. The same screen gives:

The compact option makes the 108 mm *courtyard-floored* result less sensitive to the two allowances. The table above still leaves every support passive and the enclosure walls unmodeled. ADR 0032 now selects these compact packages for provisional library/source work; this geometric result does not qualify their lands or assembly.
The compact DFF's TI RSE0008A land already exists locally and was audited for an exact USB switch sharing that package drawing; its new DFF symbol has a separate pin-table check. The new DSF/DCU/DSE/DRY source-geometry checks do not establish component-local routing or assembler stencil acceptance.

A further coarse stress case adds **twenty** 2.0 × 1.0 mm project-local 0402 courtyard boxes to the compact +1.0 mm body case and forces all twenty outside the pack outline. They represent the first support-part schedule in the [interlock proposal](../docs/design/display-interlock-circuit-proposal-2026-10-12.md): nine IC bypass capacitors and eleven divider, default, watchdog, window-pull-up and bleed resistors. This is **not** a captured or frozen BOM. The engine still adds its 0.5 mm box allowance to each. This run leaves four boxes unplaced at 106 mm and none at 108 or 110 mm. The first-fit algorithm may scatter support parts far from their ICs, so the zero count at 108 mm gives only a target for a grouped real-footprint placement. It cannot establish signal integrity, converter loops, assembly access or the 110 mm case's wall thickness.
