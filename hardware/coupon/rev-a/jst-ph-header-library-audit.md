# Staged LP452845 mating-header land, 2026-10-10

The exact Rev A battery assembly in [ADR 0024](../../../docs/decisions/0024-lp452845-pack-and-temperature-window.md) uses JST `PHR-2`; the selected board-side mating-header target is **JST `S2B-PH-SM4-TB`**, the two-circuit side-entry SMT variant. The [project-local staged footprint](footprints/rgb-badge-coupon.pretty/JST_PH_S2B-PH-SM4-TB.kicad_mod) is a first-author land with no external 3D model reference. Its contact and mounting geometry was cross-checked against KiCad 10's generated JST land and the JST drawing. It is **not assigned to a root schematic component** yet because pack-lead-to-header positive/negative mapping must be verified from the exact delivered assembly and mating view. No battery has been connected or purchased.

[JST's PH series drawing](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/ePH.pdf), pages 2 and 4, identifies the side-entry SMT body, number-1 circuit mark, 2.0 mm circuit pitch and board-layout dimensions. The staged footprint puts numbered contact pads 1 and 2 at `x=−1.0` and `+1.0 mm`, each 1.0 × 3.5 mm; pad 1 is on the marked side. Two non-electrical `MP` retention lands sit at `x=±3.35 mm`, each 1.5 × 3.4 mm. The first-author courtyard extends `x=±4.6`, `y=±5.1 mm`, matching the 9.2 × 10.2 mm envelope used in the [mechanical swap screen](../../../mechanical/lp452845-swap-screen-2026-10-09.md). Copper, mask and paste use identical lands; stencil reduction, wetting, board-edge access and retention strength require assembler review. The ePH series sheet lists 0.8–1.6 mm board thickness and a 2 A headline rating under specified wire/contact conditions; neither establishes this exact pack's continuous-current rating.

The source-level [land checker](../../../tools/check-jst-ph-library.py) fixes the two contact pad numbers, centres, sizes/layers, both retention pads, courtyard extent and lack of an external model path. KiCad CLI 10.0.6 exported the footprint's copper view to SVG and it was visually inspected. The full `tools/check-kicad.sh` now requires four native SVG views of this staged footprint. This is library preparation only; independent manufacturer pad/orientation comparison, exact cable polarity inspection, physical connector engagement and board/enclosure fit remain before an electrical pack connection or fabrication release.
