# 3.3 V converter inductor library audit — first author

2026-10-05. Exact provisional MPN: Murata `DFE252012P-1R0M=P2`, the 1 µH example in TI's [TPS631000 Rev. C datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps631000.pdf). The [Murata DFE252012P manufacturer sheet](https://www.murata.com/~/media/webrenewal/products/inductor/chip/tokoproducts/wirewoundmetalalloychiptype/m_dfe252012p.ashx) gives a 2.5 ±0.2 × 2.0 ±0.2 mm body, 1.2 mm maximum height, ±20% initial inductance and 42 mΩ maximum DCR at 20 °C. Its recommended pattern sketch shows two lands with a **2.8 mm outer span**, **1.2 mm inner gap** and **2.0 mm transverse width**. Thus each land is 0.8 × 2.0 mm, centered at x = ±1.0 mm. The project footprint reproduces these dimensions on F.Cu, F.Mask and F.Paste; pad 1 is left and pad 2 right. The inductor has no electrical polarity. `F.Fab` shows the nominal 2.5 × 2.0 mm body; the 3.3 × 2.7 mm courtyard provides 0.15 mm beyond the outer copper and 0.25 mm beyond the maximum stated body width. Courtyard and paste still need assembler review.

The project-local symbol records Murata, the exact MPN, the source sheet, two passive pins and the footprint. `tools/check-3v3-inductor-library.py` checks its source identity and measured KiCad geometry. The component is **unplaced**: this library does not validate converter connectivity or routing.

Murata gives two different current conditions for the exact part: 4.3 A at a **30% inductance decrease** and 3.2 A at a **40 °C winding temperature rise**, referenced to 20 °C ambient. Its note says the smaller applicable current controls. Neither is a guaranteed usable current in the badge's enclosure at elevated local ambient. TI's candidate 1.5 A output calculation in the [capture contract](3v3-converter-capture-contract.md) assumes efficiency, frequency and initial inductance; DC-bias inductance, winding rise, thermal coupling and layout still need bounding or measurement. Do not treat the 4.3 A figure as a blanket operating rating.

The official sheet does not supply a tolerance for its *recommended copper pattern*. It also does not qualify solder paste coverage, thermal path, footprint orientation relative to the switching loop or final pack/board height. Verify native KiCad 10.0.6 symbol and fabrication/copper/paste plots, then layout and assembler DFM. Independent Gate A review is required before fabrication.

## Owner-supplied sheet recheck, 2026-10-27

The owner supplied local `m_dfe252012p.pdf`, one page titled *Metal Alloy Power Inductors DFE252012P*. PDF metadata dates to 2016-04-22; no visible revision is printed. SHA-256: `ce259ba611fb3ea196baf60e2c7080a21cd897cdb662bf77552cc842c2b91c3e`. The official Murata product page returned access denied during this continuation, so the supplied reference was used without retrieval workarounds. It is not represented as a new manufacturer approval sheet. The PDF is excluded from Git.

The rendered drawing confirms the existing 2.8 mm outer span, 1.2 mm gap, 2.0 mm transverse lands and 2.5 ±0.2 × 2.0 ±0.2 mm body / 1.2 mm maximum height. `tools/check-3v3-inductor-library.py` passes against unchanged source geometry. L2 uses the same land pattern with exact `DFE252012P-1R5M=P2`; its table entry is 1.5 µH ±20% and 60 mΩ maximum DCR at the stated reference condition. No part substitution or footprint edit is needed.

The sheet's 1.5 µH entry lists 3.5 A for 30% inductance reduction and 2.6 A for 40 °C rise, referenced to 20 °C ambient. These are separate characterization conditions, not a raised operating limit or enclosure rating. L1/L2 are now captured and provisionally placed in an unbuilt study; the original “unplaced” statement above describes the 2026-10-05 library stage. [ADR 0054](../../../docs/decisions/0054-local-converter-inductor-placement-study.md) records the bounded local-placement investigation. No inductor pose/current change, thermal qualification or fabrication release is selected.
