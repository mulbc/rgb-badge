# Display-interlock package library audit

Date: 2026-10-12. The exact compact IC set is selected for provisional library/source capture by [ADR 0032](../../../docs/decisions/0032-two-event-display-interlock-proposal.md). This record covers **four new land patterns, eight exact IC symbols and four exact resistor symbols**. No new interlock part is placed or connected in the canonical schematic. No PCB or assembly has been tested.

| Project-local footprint | Exact selected devices | TI manufacturer land/stencil source |
|---|---|---|
| `X2SON_TI_DSF0006A_1x1mm_P0.35mm` | `SN74LVC1G04DSFR`, `SN74LVC1G08DSFR` | [DSF0006A drawing 4220597/B](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g04.pdf), June 2022, appended package pages |
| `VSSOP_TI_DCU0008A` | `SN74LVC2G00DCUR`, `SN74LVC2G08DCUR` | [DCU0008A drawing 4225266/A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ml/mpds050e/mpds050e.pdf), September 2014; same drawing appears in both gate datasheets |
| `WSON_TI_DSE0006A_1p5x1p5mm_P0p5mm` | `TLV6700DSER` | [TI comparator datasheet, DSE0006A drawing 4220552/B](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv6700.pdf), January 2024 |
| `USON_TI_DRY0006A_1p45x1mm_P0p5mm` | `SN74LVC1G11DRYR` | [TI 3-input AND datasheet, DRY0006A drawing 4222894/A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g11.pdf), January 2018 |

TI's DSF example has six 0.60 × 0.17 mm copper lands, two columns 0.8 mm apart, with 0.35 mm row pitch and 0.05 mm corner radius. Its stencil example narrows the six apertures to 0.60 × 0.15 mm for a 0.09 mm stencil. The local footprint therefore has copper/mask lands and separate paste-only apertures; it does not silently use the wider copper shape as the stencil. Pin 1 is the upper-left land in the top view. The 1 × 1 mm fabrication body and 1.9 × 1.5 mm project courtyard include at least 0.25 mm beyond pad/body extremes. The 0.05 mm NSMD mask expansion stays below TI's 0.07 mm maximum recommendation. The selected inverter's NC pins 1 and 5 must remain explicit in the schematic, not silently wired to a signal. [TI inverter pin table](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g04.pdf); [TI 2-input AND pin table](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g08.pdf).

TI's DCU example has eight 0.85 × 0.30 mm lands at 0.5 mm pitch with 3.1 mm outer horizontal span. The centres are ±1.125 mm horizontally and ±0.75/0.25 mm vertically. Its 0.125 mm stencil example uses the same aperture size. The local 2.4 × 3.2 mm fabrication outline uses the maximum package-body dimensions, while the 3.7 × 3.7 mm courtyard allows at least 0.25 mm beyond pad/body extremes. The external pin-one dot sits above-left. The two selected dual gates have the same package drawing and numbered pad order, but **not** the same logic; their separate symbols must retain the exact MPN and gate type. [TI NAND pin table](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc2g00.pdf); [TI AND pin table](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc2g08.pdf).

The DSE drawing appended to the comparator datasheet contains both the older 4220552/A land with **1.6 mm** column-centre spacing and the newer 4220552/B land with **1.2 mm** spacing. The local footprint deliberately follows the newer **B** drawing: pad 1 is 0.80 × 0.25 mm, pads 2–6 are 0.70 × 0.25 mm, rows are 0.5 mm apart and column centres are ±0.6 mm. Its 2.5 × 2.1 mm courtyard encloses the maximum 1.55 mm body and all lands by at least 0.25 mm. All six local pads use 0.05 mm non-solder-mask-defined openings; TI's example depicts pads 1–3 as solder-mask-defined and pads 4–6 as non-solder-mask-defined. This mask choice must be approved with the assembler before release, along with the 0.125 mm stencil example and fine-pitch mask webs. The **DSE** comparator pin map differs from the larger **DDC** package. [TI comparator pin table and DSE drawing](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv6700.pdf).

The DRY land uses 0.35 × 0.20 mm for pin 1 and 0.30 × 0.20 mm for pads 2–6, at ±0.3 mm column centres and 0.5 mm row pitch. The 1.6 × 2.0 mm courtyard surrounds the maximum 1.05 × 1.5 mm body by at least 0.25 mm. The 0.05 mm mask expansion follows TI's non-solder-mask-defined example; TI also shows a preferred solder-mask-defined alternative. Its 0.075–0.1 mm stencil example may conflict with the thicker stencil needed elsewhere on this board, so the common stencil and paste release require assembler review. [TI DRY package pages](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g11.pdf).

Eight exact symbols now pair `TPS3823A-33DBVR` to the existing DBV0005A land, `SN74LVC1G74RSER` to the existing RSE0008A land, the compact inverter and 2-input AND to the new DSF0006A land, the dual NAND/AND to the new DCU0008A land, and the comparator/3-input AND to the new DSE/DRY lands. `check-interlock-symbols.py` compares all **53 numbered pin functions**, electrical types, exact MPNs, datasheets and footprint references with the current TI tables. The DFF symbol explicitly distinguishes Q on pin 5 from inverted Q on pin 3, while NC pins 1/5 on the inverter and NC pin 5 on the 2-input AND are represented as no-connect pins. The comparator uses DSE-specific OUTB 1/OUTA 6 and open-drain electrical types. [TI supervisor](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3823.pdf); [TI DFF](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g74.pdf); [TI inverter](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g04.pdf); [TI 2-input AND](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g08.pdf); [TI dual NAND](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc2g00.pdf); [TI dual AND](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc2g08.pdf); [TI comparator](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv6700.pdf); [TI 3-input AND](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g11.pdf).

Four exact Panasonic 0402 resistor symbols now cover the proposed VLED window and bleed: [160 kΩ `ERJ2RKF1603X`](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/general-purpose-chip-resistors/models/ERJ2RKF1603X), [196 kΩ `ERJ2RKF1963X`](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/general-purpose-chip-resistors/models/ERJ2RKF1963X), [20 kΩ `ERJ2RKF2002X`](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/general-purpose-chip-resistors/models/ERJ2RKF2002X) and [4.7 kΩ `ERJ2RKF4701X`](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/general-purpose-chip-resistors/models/ERJ2RKF4701X). Panasonic lists each as ±1%, 0.1 W, ±100 ppm/K, 1.0 × 0.5 mm; each uses the existing project-local ERJ2 land. Existing 1 kΩ, 10 kΩ, 100 kΩ and 100 nF symbols cover the remaining first-schedule support types. The actual network is still uncaptured and its full electrical corners unqualified.

All four new footprints and all twelve new symbols rendered with local KiCad CLI 10.0.6 on 2026-10-12. The first-author DSF/DCU copper/paste/fabrication views show eight DCU pads in counter-clockwise numbering and six DSF pads plus narrower paste apertures, with pin-one dots outside copper. The DFF symbol's clock, asynchronous clear/preset and Q/inverted-Q marks were visually inspected in the native export. The DSE/DRY fabrication views show pin 1 upper left, descending left pins 1–3 and ascending right pins 4–6; their comparator/3-input AND symbols were also inspected in the native export. The footprint checker compares exact pad positions, sizes, numbers, layers, copper gaps, corner radii and courtyards with the drawings. The full local `check-kicad.sh` run **after** the DSE/DRY additions passed: zero native ERC violations and unchanged native XML at 466 PCB items/1,711 logical pins. This is source verification, not electrical function. The [fit screen](../../../mechanical/display-interlock-body-fit-2026-10-12.md) floors all nine provisional IC boxes to their courtyards; its 108 mm/20-support-box zero-miss result is unchanged. There is still no actual interlock placement or connection. Tiny DSF/DRY paste release, board finish/mask process and a common stencil thickness still need assembler review.

Next: complete the clear-net and row-off electrical review, then capture the interlock as one block. The converter EN and row decoder remain disabled in current source.
